# Phase 32 - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the detailed dendromicrohabitat (DMH) information: the new factor F group list (7 sections by form, tick, info glyph, compact -/+ stepper in full counting mode), the guide sheet (searchable catalogue, group pages, sources and licence page) and the original line drawing of each group, shown static on its page. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, to be verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Revision 2026-10-24 (owner decision D-11, amending D-06 and D-09): the 15 drawings, drawn by the assistant for the association on the style of three approved pilots, ship as vector data drawn in the theme's ink, not as bundled PNG or WebP images. The paper tile, its colour tokens, the image component, the per-drawing credit and the byte budget of image files are gone; S5, Color, the copy keys and O-32-6 are updated.
> Revision 2026-10-26 (D-11 amended by the owner): the drawings are static. The one-shot stroke animation, its timings, its hero-layer count, its Reduce Motion branch, the precomputed stroke lengths and the path geometry module are gone: react-native-svg renders the delivered path, ellipse (with its rotate transform) and circle elements directly. Design System, S5, Interaction, States, Accessibility, Motion, Registry, the implementation notes, U-13 and O-32-6 are updated.
> Sources: 32-CONTEXT.md (D-01 to D-10, locked), 32-RESEARCH.md (crosswalk, Pattern 2 and 3, levers a/b/c, pitfalls 1 to 9, gates table), 30-UI-SPEC.md (format, tokens, `GlossaryInfo` and `ChoiceCard` precedents, accessibility and motion contracts), CLAUDE.md (visual layer, text and i18n, navigation, 400-line cap), and the in-repo code read for this contract: `screens/factor-detail/DmhCountFields.tsx`, `ui/FactorCounterInput.tsx`, `ui/AbbreviationInfo.tsx`, `ui/GlossaryInfo.tsx`, `ui/AppCollapsibleSection.tsx`, `screens/FactorHelpSheet.tsx`, `i18n/fr/abbreviations.ts`, `i18n/fr/factor-input.ts` (`dmhGroupOptions`), `app/brand-tokens.ts`.
> The app is React Native (Expo). No shadcn, no Tailwind, no registry: the design system is the in-repo one (`useBrandTheme()`, `brand-tokens.ts`, `mobile/src/ui/*`). "pt" means React Native points.
> Nothing here decides an IBP rule. The guide displays the wording of the IBP sheet and computes nothing; the counts and the density stay with `useUncappedEntry` and the package. The stored payload (`F.dmh_groups`, `F.dmh_counts`, `trees_per_ha`) and SQLite version 5 do not change (D-08, CONTEXT scope).
> French sentences are drafts for the content spec `docs/specs/dmh-guide-content-v1.md` (owner plus expert reading, blocking before screens). The structure, tone, length and the chrome strings are the contract. The 15 group texts are not written here; section "Group page content template" fixes their shape.
> Choices that need the owner are tagged **[owner]** and collected in "Owner decisions". Choices inside the locked decisions that deserve a look on the phone are tagged **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**.

| sm | 8 | Gap between the checkbox zone and the info glyph; gap between stepper buttons and the count; gap between sections of the F list; gap between a label line and its stepper line |
| smd | 12 | Gap between checkbox and label; gap between stacked blocks inside a card; gap between bullet rows |
| md | 16 | Card and row horizontal padding; gap between top-level blocks of the guide |
| lg | 24 | Horizontal padding of the guide sheet body (as the help sheet and the chooser); gap before the sources link |
| xl | 32 | Space before the sources link at the foot of a page |
| xxl | 48 | Not used as a gap (48 appears only as a target size) |
| 3xl (64) | not used | |

Fixed sizes (all multiples of 4):
- **Group row (F list):** min height 56; vertical padding 8; horizontal padding 16 inside the section card. In full counting mode a ticked row adds a second line of 48 plus 8 gap (about 112 pt in total).
- **Checkbox visual:** 24 x 24, radius 8, hairline 1 pt (ticked: filled with the inverted neutral, check at 16). The press zone around it is the whole left part of the row (checkbox, 12 gap, label), never less than 56 high.
- **Info glyph:** 20 pt, hit slop to 44 x 44 (`ABBREVIATION_INFO_ICON_SIZE`), at the right end of line 1, always in the same place.
- **"+" button on an unticked row (full mode only):** 48 x 48, 8 gap to the info glyph.
- **Stepper (ticked row, full mode):** buttons 48 x 48, count 48 wide by 48 high, 8 gap each side, right-aligned on line 2, "Arbres" caption left on the same line. `DMH_STEPPER_SIZE = 48` is one constant so the owner can raise it to 56 after the glove test (**[owner]** O-32-1).
- **Guide entry row (F page):** min height 56, `AppCard` glass, padding 16.
- **Catalogue row:** min height 56, label up to three lines, trailing `chevron-forward-outline` 20.
- **Search field:** min height 48 (`AppField`), clear button 44 x 44.
- **Sheet header row:** 56 high, back or close button 44 x 44.
- **Drawing frame:** the full content width of its card, aspect ratio from the viewBox (4:3), max height 240, no tile, no border and no padding of its own (it sits inside the card padding), 12 gap to the recognition text under it. Stroke widths are in viewBox units (6 for main lines and 4 or 5 for details on an 800 wide frame, about 2.3 pt and 1.6 to 1.9 pt at 310 pt wide; dots at 7 to 12, about 2.7 to 4.6 pt; every width between 4 and 12).

Exceptions: 12 pt (`smd`, inherited `brandSpacing4` token); 1 pt hairlines; 24 pt checkbox and 8 pt checkbox radius (new, small control, same family as the 24 pt radio of the wizard); 20 pt glyph with 44 pt hit slop (inherited); 48 pt stepper buttons and count (new, between the 44 minimum and the 56 pt glove size of `FactorCounterInput`); 240 pt drawing height cap; 6 pt bullet dot of the confusion list (a text bullet, not a target); 22 / 18 radii (inherited). Every pressable target is at least 44 x 44; gaps between neighbouring targets are at least 8 so hit slops never overlap.

## Color

All values come from the theme; light and dark follow the system only.

| Role | Token | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `semanticColors.backgroundCanvas` (sheet) and the `ScreenFrame` halo (F page) | Sheet background, F page |
| Secondary (30%) | glass cards: `visual.glass.cardFill`, `cardBorder`, `cardShadow` through `AppCard variant="glass"` and `AppGroupedList` | Section cards of the F list, guide entry row, catalogue sections, every block of a group page, sources blocks |
| Accent (10%) | `visual.accentText` and `semanticColors.accent` | Reserved list below |
| Inverted neutral | `visual.chip.activeBg` with `visual.chip.activeText` | Ticked checkbox fill and its check (as the wizard radio) |
| Counter ink | `colors.forest` | `add-outline` and `remove-outline` glyphs (as `FactorCounterInput`) |
| Drawing ink | `colors.textPrimary` (existing; dark ink in light, light ink in dark) | The strokes of every drawing, drawn straight on the glass card of block 4 in both schemes; the drawing data carries no colour, so no new token and no tile |
| Destructive | not used | No destructive action |

Accent reserved for (exhaustive):
1. The info glyph (`semanticColors.accent`) on every F row, as `AbbreviationInfo` and `GlossaryInfo`.
2. The text links: "Sources et licence", "Tous les groupes", "Voir les 15 groupes" (empty search). The guide entry row title is a card title and stays in the primary ink.
3. The caret and the focus ring of the search field (the `AppField` focus state).

Other colour rules:
- A ticked row is carried by shape, not colour alone: filled checkbox with a check glyph, plus the stepper line when counting. No accent border on rows (the accent stays rare; up to 15 ticked rows would otherwise flood the screen).
- Disabled "-" at 0 uses `brandInteraction.disabledOpacity` (0.4).
- The difference block (S3.6) uses `AppNotice tone="info"`. Terracotta and warning tones are not used: the pocket guide threshold is context, not a hazard. No red text anywhere.
- The drawing follows the scheme through its ink, so it stays legible in dark mode without a paper tile. The pair "drawing ink (`colors.textPrimary`) on `glass.cardFill` over the canvas" is added to the contrast pairs of `visual-tokens.test.ts` for both schemes at 4.5:1 (thin lines, so the text threshold rather than the 3:1 of graphics). No new token and no new literal.
- No `borderWidth` on a gradient view, no `borderCurve`, no blur: nothing in this phase floats over a map.

**Where:** factor F only, in place of the companion chip list rendered by `renderCompanion` (`FactorChipsInput`, 15 chips). The numeric field "trees per hectare" above it is unchanged. Other factors keep their chips. Extract `renderFactorField` out of `FactorDetailScreen.tsx` first (research pitfall 3, the file is at the 400-line cap).

**Order top to bottom on the F page:**
1. Existing numeric field `trees_per_ha` (unchanged; in full mode it is the read-only derived value as today).
2. **Heading** (17 SemiBold): capped mode `companionLabels.dmh_groups` (existing "Groupes de dendromicrohabitats observés (facultatif)"); full mode the existing `countingMode.dmhTitle` ("Arbres par groupe de dendromicrohabitats"). Under it, full mode only, the existing helper (`dmhHelper`, 15 Regular secondary).
3. **Guide entry row** (S1.1).
4. **Summary line** (12 SemiBold, secondary, `testID="dmh-summary"`): "Aucun groupe coché." / "3 groupes cochés" (capped) / "3 groupes, 14 arbres" (full). Derived display only (a count of ticked codes and a sum of the stored counts); it is not a rule and not read by anything.
5. **Seven form sections** (S1.2), 8 apart.
6. Full mode only: the surface block that remains in `DmhCountFields` (field "Surface décrite pour ce comptage (ha)" and the "D'après le parcours" caption), unchanged in behaviour. `DmhCountFields` no longer renders counter rows; its rows move into the list.

**S1.1 Guide entry row.** `AppCard variant="glass"` pressable (`accessibilityRole="button"`), `book-outline` 24 in the card icon tile style, title (17 SemiBold) "Guide des dendromicrohabitats", caption (15 Regular secondary) "Reconnaître chaque groupe, sans réseau.", trailing `chevron-forward-outline` 20. Navigates to `surveyDmhGuide` with no param (catalogue). Present in both counting modes and in read-only mode (a submitted survey can still read the guide). `testID="dmh-guide-entry"`.

**S1.2 Sections by form.** The 7 forms partition the 15 groups (research crosswalk). One `AppCollapsibleSection` per form, title = short form name, `badge` = "{ticked} sur {total}" (12 SemiBold, always shown, "0 sur 4" included), body = one glass card holding the rows separated by 1 pt hairlines inset by 16.

| Form id | Short title on the list | Groups |
|---------|-------------------------|--------|
| cavities | Cavités | 1 to 4 |
| wounds | Blessures et bois apparent | 5, 6 |
| crown_deadwood | Bois mort dans le houppier | 7 |
| growths | Excroissances | 8, 9 |
| fruiting_bodies | Champignons et myxomycètes | 10, 11 |
| epiphytic | Plantes, nids et microsols | 12, 13, 14 |

The short titles are plain words for the field; the guide shows the official names of the pocket guide on the group page ("Forme : Structures épiphytiques, épixyliques ou parasites") **[owner]** O-32-2. A section with a single group (Bois mort dans le houppier, Exsudats) is still a section, for a predictable structure.

**Ordering and initial state: at mount only, never live (research pitfall 5).** "Mount" = the F page mounts (the pager mounts only the active page, so each arrival on F recomputes it). Pure function `orderDmhRows` in `app/dmh-guide/order.ts`:
1. Sections that hold at least one ticked or counted group first, in form order; then the other sections in form order.
2. Inside a section, ticked or counted groups first, then the rest, each in IBP order.
3. Sections with a ticked or counted group start open; when nothing is ticked, all seven start open (the observer sees every group; a vertical scroll is cheaper than seven header taps); when something is ticked, the sections without a tick start closed **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**.
4. Ticking, counting or unticking never moves a row or a section until the page is left and entered again. A section the observer opens or closes stays as they set it.
5. The stored `dmh_groups` order is untouched: it stays in option order (`selectionOptionsFor`), whatever the display order.
"Frequent first" is not implemented: no reliable frequency source exists (research lever c); the data-driven part (ticked first) is the lever.

**S1.3 Row anatomy (`DmhGroupRow`).**

Line 1 (all modes), left to right:
- **Check zone** (one `AppPressable`, `accessibilityRole="checkbox"`, flex 1, min height 56): checkbox 24, 12 gap, label (15 Regular, wraps, never truncates). Tap toggles the tick (`feedback.selection()`).
- **Info glyph** (own pressable, 44 x 44 target, `information-circle-outline` 20 accent): opens `surveyDmhGuide { group: code }`. It never toggles the tick.
- **"+" button** (full mode, unticked row only): 48 x 48, `add-outline` 22 `colors.forest`, glass control fill, 8 gap to the info glyph.

Line 2 (full mode, ticked or counted row only, 8 below line 1, indented 36 to align with the label): caption "Arbres" (12 SemiBold secondary) on the left, then `DmhCounterStepper` right-aligned: "-" 48, count 48, "+" 48.

States of one row:

| State | Capped mode | Full counting mode |
|-------|-------------|--------------------|
| Unticked | checkbox empty, label, info | checkbox empty, label, info, "+" |
| Ticked, no count | checkbox filled | checkbox filled, line 2 with count "0", "-" disabled |
| Ticked with count n > 0 | not possible (no counts in capped mode) | checkbox filled and locked, line 2 with count n |
| Read-only (submitted survey) | tick not pressable, `accessibilityState.disabled` | no "+", no stepper buttons, count shown as text; the info glyph and the guide entry stay active |
| Route not yet read (full mode undecided) | tick works | tick works, no "+" yet; the "+" appears when the route is known, no skeleton, no jump of the label |

**Stepper behaviour (`DmhCounterStepper`).** Same engine as `FactorCounterInput`: press in applies one step, holding repeats every 120 ms with the same acceleration (x2 after 6 ticks, x5 after 15), bounds 0 to 999, release stops. Tap on the count opens an inline numeric field (`keyboardType="numeric"`, comma accepted, committed on blur or submit, `inputAccessoryViewButtonLabel` "Terminé" as the existing field). The first "+" on an unticked row ticks the group and sets the count to 1 in one batch (form action `bumpDmhGroup(code, delta)`, tested in `useSurveyForm.test.ts`); no new stored key. "-" at 1 gives 0 and the group stays ticked (D-10). "-" at 0 is disabled (opacity 0.4, `accessibilityState.disabled`).

**Untick rule.** Tapping the check zone of a ticked group with count 0 unticks it. With count above 0 the tick is locked (a counted group keeps its row, research constraint): the tap shows a one-line caption under the row for 4 s (12 SemiBold, secondary, live region) "Ramenez le nombre à 0 pour décocher ce groupe." and does nothing else. No data is ever deleted by a tick tap **[owner]** O-32-3.

**Mis-tap protection.** Rows do not move under the finger (ordering at mount only). Growth happens only below the touched row (the stepper line appears under the row that was just ticked, so the next press is in the same row, 52 pt lower). The "+" on an unticked row and the stepper "+" are never both visible. Adjacent targets are 8 apart.

**Performance contract.** Rows are memoised on (code, ticked, count, editable, mode). One stepper tap re-renders one row (research pitfall 8). Checked on the phone with scenario S2 of the timing protocol.

### S3. The group page

Order top to bottom, horizontal padding 24, gap 16, each block an `AppCard variant="glass"` unless noted. Content comes from the group's catalogue entry (template in "Group page content template"); the group label comes from `selectionLabel("F", code)`.

1. **Title block** (no card): form caption (12 SemiBold secondary) "Forme : {official form name}", then the group label (24 SemiBold, `accessibilityRole="header"`).
2. **"De quoi s'agit-il ?"** - the definition, 15 Regular, at most four lines of source text.
3. **"Ce qui compte pour l'IBP"** - the counting rule of the IBP sheet, `leaf-outline` 18 beside the title, rule text 15 Regular; the key number or size is in the same weight (no bold run: two weights only). When the survey's method version has a distinct wording for this group (groups 6, 12 and 15 only, research v3.0 versus v3.2), the card shows the wording of the survey's version and under it a 12 SemiBold caption "Texte pour la méthode IBP version 3.0" or "version 3.2". The method comes from `useSurveyFormState().state.ibpMethodVersion` (`null` = version 3.0).
4. **"Comment le reconnaître"** - the group's drawing first (`DmhDrawing`, S5; nothing at all when the group has no data in the drawings module), then the recognition text (15 Regular, at most six lines).
5. **"Confusions fréquentes"** - 2 to 4 short bullets (dot 6 pt in `colors.textMuted`, 12 gap, 15 Regular), each states what it is confused with and where it goes ("Une simple éraflure de l'écorce ne compte pas." / "Un bois dégagé par un éclatement du tronc va dans le groupe 6.").
6. **"Types décrits par le guide de poche"** - two sub-lists when both exist: "Comptés par l'IBP" (the types inside the group) and "Décrits, mais non comptés par l'IBP" (types the pocket guide describes for this form that the IBP does not count, e.g. group 11, group 13). ### S5. The drawing (`DmhDrawing`)

Fifteen original line drawings, one per IBP group, drawn by the assistant for the association and owned by it (D-06, D-09, D-11 of 2026-10-24, amended 2026-10-26); style approved on three pilots (groups 1, 10 and 13, `drawings-pilot/`). **Data.** `app/dmh-guide/drawings.ts`, generated (never edited by hand): `DMH_DRAWINGS: Readonly<Partial<Record<DmhGroupCode, DmhDrawingData>>>`, where `DmhDrawingData = { elements: DmhDrawingElement[] }` and an element is a path `{ kind: "path"; d; width }` (the source's path data copied verbatim: react-native-svg reads relative commands, several subpaths in one path and the `h0.1` dots as the source draws them), an ellipse `{ kind: "ellipse"; cx; cy; rx; ry; width; rotate?: [angle, cx, cy] }` or a circle `{ kind: "circle"; cx; cy; r; width }`. Every width is inside `DMH_DRAWING_STROKE_RANGE` (4 to 12 viewBox units: D-11 names 4, 5, 6, 9 and 12, and the sources redrawn on 2026-10-26 also use 7, so the style is a range, not a list). Every drawing uses `DMH_DRAWING_FRAME` (800 x 600). Elements are in source order. No colour, no fill, no text in the data. `drawingFor(code)` returns the data or `null`.

| State | Behaviour |
|-------|-----------|
| Group page opens | The drawing is there, complete, with the rest of the page (it shares the view cross-fade of S2 and has no motion of its own, so there is nothing to change under Reduce Motion) |

| Loading, error | None: the data is in the JS bundle, nothing is decoded or fetched |

**Accessibility.** One element, role image, label = the group's `illustrationAlt` (one sentence describing the drawing, from the validated spec); the shapes are hidden from assistive tech.

**Budget and gate.** At most 12 KiB of data per drawing (its compact JSON text; the largest redrawn source, dmh_02 with about 146 elements, measured about 7 kB on 2026-10-26) and 120 KiB for the whole module (about 60 kB estimated). `__checks__/dmh-drawings.test.ts`: the 15 group codes and no other key, widths inside `DMH_DRAWING_STROKE_RANGE`, every element renders (well-formed path data, positive radii), both budgets, no colour in the module, and (with the catalogue) an alt sentence per drawing; every rule proven on fixtures.

## Accessibility Contract

  - Section header: button with `accessibilityState.expanded`, label "{form}, {n} groupes sur {total} cochés".
  - Summary line: plain text, not a live region (no announcement per tap; the adjustable value and the checkbox state are already announced).
  - The locked-untick caption is a polite live region (`accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` Android, `announceForAccessibility` iOS).

Colour is never the only carrier: ticked = filled box with a check; counted = the number; locked = the caption.
- **Reduced motion:** layout transitions (stepper line, sections) and the view cross-fade are instant (the drawings are static in every case); the chevron rotation of the sections is instant (existing component); no information depends on motion.
