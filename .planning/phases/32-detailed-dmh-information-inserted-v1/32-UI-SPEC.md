# Phase 32 - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the detailed dendromicrohabitat (DMH) information: the new factor F group list (7 sections by form, tick, info glyph, compact -/+ stepper in full counting mode), the guide sheet (searchable catalogue, group pages, sources and licence page) and the original line drawing of each group, shown static on its page. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, to be verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Revision 2026-10-24 (owner decision D-11, amending D-06 and D-09): the 15 drawings, drawn by the assistant for the association on the style of three approved pilots, ship as vector data drawn in the theme's ink, not as bundled PNG or WebP images. The paper tile, its colour tokens, the image component, the per-drawing credit and the byte budget of image files are gone; S5, Color, the copy keys and O-32-6 are updated.
> Revision 2026-10-26 (D-11 amended by the owner): the drawings are static. The one-shot stroke animation, its timings, its hero-layer count, its Reduce Motion branch, the precomputed stroke lengths and the path geometry module are gone: react-native-svg renders the delivered path, ellipse (with its rotate transform) and circle elements directly. Design System, S5, Interaction, States, Accessibility, Motion, Registry, the implementation notes, U-13 and O-32-6 are updated.
> Revision 2026-10-28 (owner feedback "l'interface fait un peu web", brief `32-REDESIGN-BRIEF.md`, owner decisions at its end; plans 32-18a, 32-18c, 32-19): the F list and the guide sheet are redrawn as iOS-style grouped lists. One container level (`AppGroupedContainer`) straight on the canvas, grouped section headers, a selection circle instead of the checkbox square, a single-capsule stepper, readable glyph inks (WCAG 1.4.11), a restyled sticky sheet bar with a push transition, a calm group page with one tinted callout and the drawing on a moss plate at the top. The rows changed below carry "(2026-10-28)". No copy, data, payload or route changes; `visual.control.fill` is the one new token (owner approved).
> Sources: 32-CONTEXT.md (D-01 to D-10, locked), 32-RESEARCH.md (crosswalk, Pattern 2 and 3, levers a/b/c, pitfalls 1 to 9, gates table), 30-UI-SPEC.md (format, tokens, `GlossaryInfo` and `ChoiceCard` precedents, accessibility and motion contracts), CLAUDE.md (visual layer, text and i18n, navigation, 400-line cap), and the in-repo code read for this contract: `screens/factor-detail/DmhCountFields.tsx`, `ui/FactorCounterInput.tsx`, `ui/AbbreviationInfo.tsx`, `ui/GlossaryInfo.tsx`, `ui/AppCollapsibleSection.tsx`, `screens/FactorHelpSheet.tsx`, `i18n/fr/abbreviations.ts`, `i18n/fr/factor-input.ts` (`dmhGroupOptions`), `app/brand-tokens.ts`.
> The app is React Native (Expo). No shadcn, no Tailwind, no registry: the design system is the in-repo one (`useBrandTheme()`, `brand-tokens.ts`, `mobile/src/ui/*`). "pt" means React Native points.
> Nothing here decides an IBP rule. The guide displays the wording of the IBP sheet and computes nothing; the counts and the density stay with `useUncappedEntry` and the package. The stored payload (`F.dmh_groups`, `F.dmh_counts`, `trees_per_ha`) and SQLite version 5 do not change (D-08, CONTEXT scope).
> French sentences are drafts for the content spec `docs/specs/dmh-guide-content-v1.md` (owner plus expert reading, blocking before screens). The structure, tone, length and the chrome strings are the contract. The 15 group texts are not written here; section "Group page content template" fixes their shape.
> Choices that need the owner are tagged **[owner]** and collected in "Owner decisions". Choices inside the locked decisions that deserve a look on the phone are tagged **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**.

- **Info button (2026-10-28):** glyph 22 pt in a 44 x 44 target, at the right end of line 1, always in the same place.
- **Add control on an unticked row (full mode only):** a 48 x 48 target around a 36 pt disc, 8 gap to the info button.
- **Stepper (ticked row, full mode) (2026-10-28):** one 112 x 48 capsule of two 56 x 48 segments, the plain count (min 44 x 48) 8 to its left, right-aligned on line 2, "Arbres" caption left on the same line. `DMH_STEPPER_SIZE = 48` is one constant so the owner can raise it to 56 after the glove test (**[owner]** O-32-1).
- **Guide entry row (F page):** min height 56, `AppCard` glass, padding 16.
- **Catalogue row:** min height 56, label up to three lines, trailing `chevron-forward-outline` 20.
- **Search field:** min height 48 (`AppField`), clear button 44 x 44.
- **Sheet header row:** 56 high, back or close button 44 x 44.
- **Drawing frame:** the full content width of its card, aspect ratio from the viewBox (4:3), max height 240, no tile, no border and no padding of its own (it sits inside the card padding), 12 gap to the recognition text under it. Every pressable target is at least 44 x 44; gaps between neighbouring targets are at least 8 so hit slops never overlap.

**S1.3 Row anatomy (`DmhGroupRow`).**

Line 1 (all modes), left to right (2026-10-28):
- **Check zone** (one `AppPressable`, `accessibilityRole="checkbox"`, flex 1, min height 56, pressed fill `colors.surfaceSoft`, no ripple): `SelectionCircle` 24 (ring `colors.inputBorder`, ticked `ctaPrimary` fill with an outline check), 12 gap, label (`heroBody`, Sora-Medium 15/21, wraps, never truncates). - **Add control** (full mode, unticked row only): a 48 x 48 target holding a 36 pt disc in `visual.control.fill` with `add-outline` 20 in `ctaPrimary`, 8 gap to the info button.

Line 2 (full mode, ticked or counted row only, 8 below line 1, indented 52 to align with the label): caption "Arbres" (12 SemiBold secondary) on the left, then `DmhCounterStepper` right-aligned: the count as plain text (Sora-SemiBold 20/25, min 44 x 48; a tap opens the inline field, 2 pt underline while editing), 8 gap, then one 112 x 48 pill capsule in `visual.control.fill` with two 56 x 48 segments (`remove-outline`, `add-outline` 22 in `ctaPrimary`) and a 1 x 24 divider in `colors.divider` between them.

States of one row:

| State | Capped mode | Full counting mode |
|-------|-------------|--------------------|
| Unticked | checkbox empty, label, info | checkbox empty, label, info, "+" |
| Ticked, no count | checkbox filled | checkbox filled, line 2 with count "0", "-" disabled |
| Ticked with count n > 0 | not possible (no counts in capped mode) | checkbox filled and locked, line 2 with count n |

**Stepper behaviour (`DmhCounterStepper`).** Same engine as `FactorCounterInput` (the capsule look is the 2026-10-28 one above; the pressed segment fills with `colors.surfaceSoft`, no scale; `feedback.selection()` on each single press in of "-" or "+", not on repeats or on "-" at 0): press in applies one step, holding repeats every 120 ms with the same acceleration (x2 after 6 ticks, x5 after 15), bounds 0 to 999, release stops. Tap on the count opens an inline numeric field (`keyboardType="numeric"`, comma accepted, committed on blur or submit, `inputAccessoryViewButtonLabel` "Terminé" as the existing field). The first "+" on an unticked row ticks the group and sets the count to 1 in one batch (form action `bumpDmhGroup(code, delta)`, tested in `useSurveyForm.test.ts`); no new stored key. "-" at 1 gives 0 and the group stays ticked (D-10). "-" at 0 is disabled (its glyph at `brandInteraction.disabledOpacity`, `accessibilityState.disabled`).

**Untick rule.** Tapping the check zone of a ticked group with count 0 unticks it. With count above 0 the tick is locked (a counted group keeps its row, research constraint): the tap shows a one-line caption under the row for 4 s (12 SemiBold, secondary, live region) "Ramenez le nombre à 0 pour décocher ce groupe." and does nothing else. No data is ever deleted by a tick tap **[owner]** O-32-3.

**Mis-tap protection.** Rows do not move under the finger (ordering at mount only). Growth happens only below the touched row (the stepper line appears under the row that was just ticked, so the next press is in the same row, 52 pt lower). The "+" on an unticked row and the stepper "+" are never both visible. Adjacent targets are 8 apart.

**Performance contract.** Rows are memoised on (code, ticked, count, editable, mode). One stepper tap re-renders one row (research pitfall 8). Checked on the phone with scenario S2 of the timing protocol.

## Decisions taken in this contract (reasoning)

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|----------|--------|
| U-01 | The chip list of F becomes a row list in 7 form sections; the other factors keep their chips | A chip cannot carry a separate info button or a stepper; the 7 forms partition the 15 groups, so grouping is free and halves scrolling (lever b) |
| U-02 | The stepper sits on a second line of a ticked row, the "+" on an unticked row's first line | A 15-character-wide label needs the width; the first "+" ticks and counts in one tap (lever a); the stepper never moves under the thumb once visible |
| U-03 | Tick and info are separate targets; the check zone is the whole left part of the row | Large glove target for the frequent action; the info action cannot toggle by accident |
| U-04 | A counted group cannot be unticked until its count is 0 | No silent data loss and no count on an unticked group, which the payload never holds |
| U-05 | Ordering only at mount | Rows that move under the finger cause mis-taps (research pitfall 5) |
| U-06 | Search lives in the guide catalogue, not on the F page | A screen holds about 10 rows; grouping already narrows the list; search is useful for finding a group by a common name (pic, lierre, gui) |
| U-07 | One sheet route with three internal views | Avoids a form sheet over a form sheet on iOS (untested here); one registration in the survey stack |
| U-08 | "Ce qui compte pour l'IBP" is the first block after the definition and the guide threshold appears only in a labelled difference notice | Observers must count like the IBP sheet (research pitfall 1) |
| U-09 | The guide is read-only: no "cocher ce groupe" action on a group page | One write path for F; avoids a second place that edits the payload |
| U-10 | No spinner, no loading state, no offline banner | Everything is in the bundle; Phase 30 precedent for offline silence |
| U-11 | A group without drawing data renders no slot | All 15 drawings ship (D-11); the rule keeps a missing entry harmless instead of showing an "à venir" tile |
| U-12 | Typography 24 / 17 / 15 / 12 with two weights, spacing on the 4-grid with the listed exceptions | Existing roles |
| U-13 | Drawings are vector data in the theme's ink, shown static, not bundled images on a paper tile | Owner decisions 2026-10-24 and 2026-10-26: 1 to 7 kB of data per drawing instead of image files, sharp at every size, legible in dark mode without a tile; the owner tried a stroke-drawing preview and a looping preview and dropped both as useless |
