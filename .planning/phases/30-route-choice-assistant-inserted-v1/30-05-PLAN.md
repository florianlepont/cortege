<objective>
Encode the owner-validated route-choice rules in `@cortege/ibp-domain` as pure, text-free functions: which questions are still needed, the suggested parcours and counting with their reason, warning and advice codes, the two options above the subdivision bound, the suggested surface décrite (with the sum of several placettes, R-15), the live checks of that surface, the default counting, the presets of the relevé déplafonné and the advice of a selection (duration, available time, beginner, longer relevé). The decision table becomes data (`ROUTE_RULE_CASES`), checked against the spec's id list.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 2 (a guided flow suggests the route from the parcel's characteristics) rests on these functions; CLAUDE.md forbids any rule in a screen.
Output: `route/guide.ts`, `route/cases.ts`, their tests at 100 %, the API-side spec consistency test.

Owner decisions: read `30-02-SUMMARY.md` and sections 4 to 6 and 11 of the validated spec first. The spec is the source of every code, constant and threshold (O-02 10 and 20 ha, O-03 subdivide pre-selected, O-04 time and experience as information, O-05 5 ha, O-10 no linear-stand code, O-11 warning under 0.5 ha, O-14 two parcours, O-18 no objective question, O-20 placettes summed on one sheet, U-O11 5000 ha); a constant deviation recorded at the gate replaces a value, named in this plan's SUMMARY. CONTEXT D-02 (every method of the official text) is superseded by O-14: there is no typological or systematic value anywhere in the package.
</objective>
