<objective>
Encode the owner-validated route-choice rules in `@cortege/ibp-domain` as pure, text-free functions: which questions are still needed, the suggested parcours and counting with their reason, warning and advice codes, the alternatives above the subdivision bound, the suggested surface décrite, the live checks of that surface, the default counting per parcours, the lifting presets and the duration estimate. The decision table becomes data (`ROUTE_RULE_CASES`), checked against the spec's id list.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 2 (a guided flow suggests the route from the parcel's characteristics) rests on these functions; CLAUDE.md forbids any rule in a screen. UI-SPEC "Implementation notes for the planner" lists what the UI needs from the package.
Output: `route/guide.ts`, `route/cases.ts`, their tests at 100 %, the API-side spec consistency test.

Owner decisions: read section 11 of the validated spec and the `deviations:` of 30-02-SUMMARY.md first. Implement the validated rule table and constants exactly; the defaults quoted in this plan (O-02 10 and 20 ha, O-03 subdivide pre-selected, O-04 advice only, O-05 5 ha, O-10 no linear-stand code, O-11 warn under 0.5 ha, U-O11 5000 ha) give way to a validated value, named in this plan's SUMMARY.
</objective>
