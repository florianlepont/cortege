Owner decisions: section 7 and U-O10 of the validated spec (default: detail keys never scored; the scored key is derived while the factor is counted in full and kept, editable, when it leaves the full count).

Constraints: `useSurveyForm.ts` is a hook (no 400-line cap) but already large: the new state lives in the new hook. Do not edit `mobile/src/hooks/useSurveySync.ts`.
