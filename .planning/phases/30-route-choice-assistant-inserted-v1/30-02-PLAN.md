<objective>
The owner gate of the phase (D-10, ROADMAP criterion 1): the owner reads the written spec, resolves every pending decision (O-01 to O-16, U-O1 to U-O15) and validates the spec in writing with the date; Claude records the reply verbatim in the spec. The same gate is the first D-04 decision point: the owner confirms that the pooled group calculation moves to a Phase 30.1 (recommended) or keeps it in Phase 30 against the written override criteria.

Purpose: nothing that encodes a rule or builds a screen may start before the owner has validated the rules. Every code and screen plan of the phase depends on this plan.
Output: the validated spec, and 30-02-SUMMARY.md with `validated_on:`, `d04:` and `deviations:` lines that every later plan reads.

D-04 handling (planned): if the owner confirms the split, Phase 30 continues as planned and only records the data Phase 30.1 needs; the close-out plan 30-23 asks the owner a second time, with the built app in hand. If the owner keeps the group calculation in Phase 30, this plan stops with "PHASE REPLAN NEEDED: group calculation lot (research 2.4, 6 to 8 plans)" and the orchestrator re-plans before any later plan runs.

Constraints: Claude never validates on the owner's behalf and never fills a "Décision validée" cell without the owner's written answer; "défaut" in the owner's reply counts as an answer. Do not edit `.planning/STATE.md`, `.planning/ROADMAP.md` or `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`.
</objective>
