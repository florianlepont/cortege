<objective>
Assemble the route step screen and make it the mandatory last step of survey creation: after the parcel map, the step opens with the computed surface; the survey is finalised only when its route is accepted; going back returns to the parcel map. The same route serves the edit mode reached from the chooser.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 2, O-19 and U-O2 (mandatory from creation; CONTEXT D-06 "skippable" is superseded), open point P-1 (finalised when the route is accepted), D-08 (the route recorded with the survey), spec section 1 (offline, SEED-007).
Output: `RouteGuideScreen`, `RouteGuideRoute`, `finishSurveyCreation`, the stack registration and param types, the parcel route change, the five-step wizard count, navigation tests.

Owner decisions: O-09 (the step applies to v3.0 and v3.2 alike), U-O14 (nothing saved before acceptance), P-1 default from `30-02-SUMMARY.md`. UI-SPEC precedence: superseded in its sections 1, 2 and the Interaction Contract: the optional tag, the skip link, the skip on Android back, the hidden back button and disabled swipe (they protected a draft that, under P-1, is not finalised yet), and "not a numbered step" (the creation now counts five steps: the wizard's three, the parcel map, the route step).

Why the draft is not finalised at the parcel map any more: the creation assistant's working draft exists on the phone from its first step and is saved automatically (`useEditingDraft`); it stays on the phone until it has a route (30-16). Finalising it only at acceptance means back from the route step is a plain return to the parcel map, with the editing session still open.

Constraints: the chooser screen is registered by 30-21; this plan already declares its params so "Je connais déjà mon parcours" and "Changer de parcours" navigate to it. Never call `useSurveySync`; do not edit `mobile/src/hooks/useSurveySync.ts`; `shouldHideTabBar` is unchanged. Phase 29 changed `SurveysStack.tsx`, `types.ts` and `routes.test.tsx` after planning: read the current files.
</objective>
