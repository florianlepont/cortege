<objective>
Give the survey form the detail inputs of the relevé déplafonné for B and F as a sub-hook (`useUncappedEntry`, on the `useFactorAnnotations` model), write them into the factor payload with their scored keys, load them from a draft, and publish their setters in the form context for the factor screens (30-19).

Purpose: D-07 (the counting mode adapts the form) without changing any plafonné entry or score (D-09). CONTEXT D-03 (data pooled by a group calculation) is superseded by O-14: the details serve the single sheet only.
Output: `useUncappedEntry` and its integration in `useSurveyForm`, context actions, regression tests (autosave, reload, completion counters, code parity with the package).

Owner decisions: section 7 and U-O10 of the validated spec (detail keys never scored; the scored key is derived while the factor is counted in full and kept, editable, when it leaves the full count). F's own `surface_ha` is prefilled from the route's surface décrite on the F page (30-19, through the prefill hook of 30-15) and stays editable.

Constraints: `useSurveyForm.ts` is a hook (no 400-line cap) but already large: the new state lives in the new hook. Do not edit `mobile/src/hooks/useSurveySync.ts`. Read the current code (phase 29 changed the editing hooks after planning).
</objective>
