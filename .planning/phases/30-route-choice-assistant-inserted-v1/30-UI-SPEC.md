# Phase 30 - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the route choice assistant: the skippable guide step of survey creation (questionnaire, suggestion, direct choice), the "Parcours utilisé" display (Contexte page and PDF), the déplafonné entry fields of the factor screens and the way to change the route of a draft. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Sources: 30-CONTEXT.md (D-01 to D-10, locked), 30-RESEARCH.md (rule table R-01 to R-21, questionnaire 1.4, draft reasons 1.5, glossary 7.2, wording principles 7.3, tester test 7.4, owner decisions O-01 to O-16, pitfalls 1, 2, 7, 8, 10, 11), 28-UI-SPEC.md (format precedent), CLAUDE.md (Visual layer, Text and i18n, abbreviation and dash gates, 400-line cap), charter section 13, and the in-repo UI code read for this contract: `survey-wizard/SurveyWizardScreen.tsx` and `wizard.styles.ts` (choice cards, floating footer, step counter), `ui/CasPicker.tsx`, `ui/AppChoiceChip.tsx`, `ui/AppCollapsibleSection.tsx`, `ui/AppNotice.tsx`, `ui/AbbreviationInfo.tsx`, `ui/ForestCard.tsx`, `SurveyContextScreen.tsx`, `survey-detail/conditions/ConditionsSection.tsx`, `survey-pdf/conditions-section.ts`, `survey-pdf/identity.ts`, `FactorDetailScreen.tsx`, `survey-form/FactorPager.tsx`, `survey-form/usePagerKeyboard.ts`, `navigation/tab-bar.ts`, `i18n/fr/abbreviations.ts`, `__checks__/abbreviations.test.ts`.
> The app is React Native (Expo). No shadcn, no Tailwind, no registry: the design system is the in-repo one (`useBrandTheme()`, `brand-tokens.ts`, `mobile/src/ui/*`). "pt" means React Native points.
> Nothing here decides a method rule. Every question, threshold, route, counting mode, reason and warning comes from `@cortege/ibp-domain` (`recommendRoute` and its companions); screens only render what the package returns. French sentences are drafts for the owner's wording review (30-13); their structure, tone and length are the contract.
> Choices that need the owner are tagged **[owner]** and collected in "Owner decisions pending". Choices inside the locked decisions that deserve a look on the phone are tagged **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**.

**Vocabulary (owner decision O-12, research pitfalls 1 and 2; binds every string):**
- **"Parcours"** is the route (the CNPF sheet's "Méthode"). It is never called "méthode" in a user string: "Méthode IBP" stays the version (v3.2 or v3.0) of the existing wizard question.
- **"Comptage"** is the counting mode. The two values are written **"Comptage rapide"** and **"Comptage complet"**, each followed once, in brackets, by the official word the observer will meet in the documents: "(relevé plafonné)" and "(relevé déplafonné)". **[owner]** U-O1. The word "plafonné" alone is never used for the counting mode and "plafonné" is never reused in a score line: factor A's "Score plafonné à 2 si le couvert ... est inférieur à 50 %" is a different thing, and the tester test checks the confusion (30-15).
- **"Peuplement"** is defined on the guide screen before it is used (section 1, lead sentence and the glossary).
- The Centre national de la propriété forestière is never written "CNPF" in a string (the abbreviation gate rejects it): the texts say "la méthode officielle de l'IBP".

## Layout and component contract

### 1. Entry into the guide (S1) and navigation

- **Where:** after the parcel choice. `ParcelSelectionRoute.onSave` calls `createDraft()` as today; instead of resetting at once it navigates to `surveyRouteGuide` with `{ surveyId, surfaceHa: number | null }`, then the guide's two exits ("Utiliser ce parcours", "Passer cette étape") end with the existing `navigation.reset` and `openSurvey(createdId)` sequence (the summary screen's `beforeRemove` keeps the selection, `wizard-reset-flow.test.tsx` must still pass). The guide is **not** a numbered wizard step: the wizard stays "Étape 4 sur 4", the guide shows a small `Facultatif` tag under its title and no progress bar. **[owner]** U-O2. Reason: creation must feel short (SEED-007); a fifth numbered step would lengthen it.
- **Back:** the draft already exists, so the guide offers no way back to the parcel map: native back button hidden (`headerBackVisible: false`), swipe-back disabled (`gestureEnabled: false`), hardware back on Android = "Passer cette étape". The two exits are always on screen.
- **Header:** native large title "Aide au choix du parcours" on iOS (transparent header over the halo, D-19/D-30), `PageTitle` on Android; no header buttons (the exits are in the footer).
- **Creation is never blocked:** nothing on this screen requires an answer to leave. Any failure to read geometry, surface or catalogue leaves "Passer cette étape" working (SEED-007).
- **Entry from Contexte (S6):** `surveyRouteChoose` (mode `edit`) from the "Parcours utilisé" card; its "Être conseillé" link opens `surveyRouteGuide` (mode `edit`, answers loaded from the record when present, no reset sequence: it returns to Contexte).

### 3. The question stack (progressive disclosure)

> 2026-10-14 simplification after owner review: the first view holds only the title, "Étape 5 sur 5", the one-sentence lead and the open question (its field or choices and its "Suivant"). Every secondary help (reason it is asked, hectare comparison, hedge and tree-line limit, estimate explanation) sits in one information sheet opened by the (i) beside the question title. "Pour affiner" shows only once a suggestion exists. "Je connais déjà mon parcours" is a small text link at the end of the scroll content. The "Utiliser ce parcours" footer row is not rendered while questions are open (no disabled button). Result order: suggestion, "Changer de parcours", reasons, details, notices, "Pour affiner", glossary, link. Where the text below says otherwise, this note wins.


**Rule:** the observer sees exactly **one open question card** at a time. A question already answered collapses to an **answered row** (caption = short question, value = the answer, trailing "Modifier"); a question not needed (or not reached) is not drawn. Tapping an answered row reopens it and collapses the open one. Changing an answer recomputes the next needed questions and the suggestion; answers to questions that are no longer needed are kept in state (so changing back loses nothing) but are not recorded unless still needed.

**Which questions are needed comes from the package, not from the screen.** The package exposes a pure function (proposed name `routeQuestionsFor(answers)`) that returns the ordered ids of the questions to show given the answers so far, and `recommendRoute(answers)` once they are complete. The screen never contains an `if (surface > 1 ...)`. (Research 1.4 gives the dependencies below; the spec numbers them once.)

| Id | Title (card) | Control | Shown when (from the package) | Default | Advance |
|----|--------------|---------|------------------------------|---------|---------|
| purpose | "Pourquoi faites-vous ce relevé ?" | 4 `ChoiceCard` radios | always | "Décrire un peuplement en entier", tagged "Le plus courant" | the card has "Suivant" (17 SemiBold link-button, 44 high) because the default is already selected; tapping another choice advances at once |
| surface | "Quelle est la surface du peuplement ?" | `AppField` numeric (decimal comma accepted), suffix "ha" | always | prefilled from the parcels (editable); empty if unknown | "Suivant" button in the card; `returnKeyType="done"` triggers it |
| homogeneity | "Le peuplement est-il homogène ?" | 2 `ChoiceCard` radios | surface above 1 ha and purpose "whole stand" or a typological case | none | tap advances |
| difficulty | "Est-il difficile à observer ?" | 2 `ChoiceCard` radios | surface 1 to 5 ha, or a partial route | none | tap advances |
| narrow | "Le peuplement fait-il moins de 100 mètres de large ?" | 2 `ChoiceCard` radios | partial route only | none | tap advances |
| refine | "Pour affiner (facultatif)" | `AppCollapsibleSection` collapsed by default: experience chips ("Oui, j'en ai déjà fait" / "Non, c'est nouveau pour moi") and a numeric field "Temps disponible, en heures" | always, but folded and never required | none | none (no "Suivant"; the content is advice only, O-04) |

- Each open card has, under its title, a **one-sentence "why"** in 15 Regular secondary (always visible, not behind a tap: the owner's main concern is understanding). Each choice has a **caption with an example** (7.3). No glossary word appears in a title before its caption or "why" has defined it.
- Typical routine: purpose confirmed (1 tap), surface confirmed (1 tap), homogeneity (1 tap), "Utiliser ce parcours" (1 tap): four taps. A stand of 1 ha or less: three taps. A tester who does not know the method can read the "why" of each card without leaving the screen.
- The surface card, when prefilled, shows the caption "Estimée d'après les parcelles que vous avez choisies. Corrigez-la si votre peuplement est plus petit ou plus grand." (a cadastral parcel is not a stand: research pitfall 10), and an aid line "Un hectare, c'est un carré de 100 mètres de côté."
- When the surface is invalid, the field shows the error under it (`AppField error`), "Suivant" stays disabled, nothing else changes.
- The **refine** section never appears in the answered rows unless filled. It never changes the suggested parcours; it can add an advice notice (section 4, "Conseils").

**Answered row** (`testID="route-answer-{id}"`): `accessibilityRole="button"`, label "{question courte}. {réponse}. Modifier", hint "Rouvre la question". Value = the choice title (or "2,4 ha, estimée" for a prefilled surface).

## Accessibility Contract

- **Targets:** every pressable at least 44 x 44: `ChoiceCard` (min 64), answered row (min 56), links (44 high), chips (44), the info glyph (hit slop to 44), switch row (min 56), "Suivant" (44), footer button (`GlassButton lg`), the percent fields and counter steppers (min 48 high).
- **Roles:** each question's choice group is a `radiogroup` of `radio` cards with `selected` state, label "{titre}. {légende}" (`a11y.optionLabel`); the answered row is a `button` ("{question}. {réponse}. Modifier", hint "Rouvre la question"); the question title is `header`; the open card announces its title when it opens (focus moves with `AccessibilityInfo.setAccessibilityFocus`); the suggestion forest card is one element reading "Parcours conseillé : {nom}. {comptage}. Surface décrite {n} hectares." with the link "Changer de parcours" as its own button; the reasons card title is `header` and each reason one text element; `Switch` rows have `accessibilityRole="switch"`, label, hint and `checked`; the factor-status chip is non-interactive text "Comptage complet"; decorative glyphs hidden from accessibility.
- **Announcements:** the suggestion appearing or changing (polite, once per change, `a11y.suggestionChanged`); warnings and the save error as live regions (`accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` Android, `announceForAccessibility` iOS); a new question opening is announced by the focus move.
- **Dynamic Type:** no clipped text at AX3 in the guide, chooser, Contexte card and factor fields; cards grow; the footer stays a floating bar over the scroll content (content bottom padding follows the measured footer height). The forest card's text blocks are re-measured on font scale change (`blocks`).
- **Screen reader order:** title, lead, "Je connais déjà mon parcours", the answered rows in order, the open question, the suggestion (card, reasons, notices, details), the glossary section, then the footer ("Utiliser ce parcours", "Passer cette étape").
- **Small phone (320 x 568 and 360 x 640):** horizontal padding 24 leaves 272 pt of content; a `ChoiceCard` text column is about 220 pt after the radio; one open question plus the answered rows and the lead fit without scrolling at default type on 568 pt tall screens except the suggestion, which scrolls; the footer is 56 + 8 + 44 plus the safe-area padding and never covers the open card's "Suivant" (the content padding is the footer height). Checked on the small simulators and recorded in `docs/user-tests/device-checks.md`.
- **Keyboard:** numeric fields use `decimal-pad` (iOS has no "done" key on it: the card's "Suivant" button and the field's `onSubmitEditing` both exist, and a "Terminé" accessory is not added); the footer fades while the keyboard is up; the focused field is scrolled into view; the scroll never jumps when the footer reappears.
- **Contrast:** all text on `glass.cardFill` in both schemes is already covered by the glass tests; the forest card's new blocks are added to `forest-aurora-tokens.test.ts` (4.5:1 text, 3:1 graphics); the accent text links and the info glyph are covered by `visual-tokens.test.ts` pairs (`accentText` on the glass card, `accent` on the glass card), extended if the guide uses a new pair. Colour is never the only carrier: the selected card has the 2 pt border and the filled radio with a check; the suggested parcours in the chooser has the word "Conseillé".
- **Reduced motion:** layout transitions, entrances and the chevron rotation are instant; the forest aurora follows its own `ReduceMotion` handling (still); no information depends on motion.

## Decisions taken in this contract (reasoning)

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|----------|--------|
| U-01 | The guide is one scrolling screen with an accordion of questions (one open at a time), not one screen per question | A fifth wizard screen would lengthen creation (SEED-007); the accordion keeps the whole story visible (answers above, result below) and a routine run is four taps |
| U-02 | Question visibility and the suggestion come from the package (`routeQuestionsFor`, `recommendRoute`), the screen holds no threshold | CLAUDE.md: rules only in `@cortege/ibp-domain`; testable at the 100 % bar |
| U-03 | The "why" of each question and the example in each choice are always visible, not behind a tap | The owner's main concern is that a person who does not know the method completes the choice unaided (criterion 4, 7.3) |
| U-04 | The guide is unnumbered, tagged "Facultatif", with no way back to the parcel map | The draft exists once the parcels are saved; a back route would duplicate drafts; the two exits are always visible |
| U-05 | The suggestion is a `ForestCard` (the single signature moment of the screen) with a separate glass reasons card | Short text on the aurora stays readable; the long reasons sit on a calm glass card |
| U-06 | "Changer de parcours" and "Je connais déjà mon parcours" both open the same chooser; the chooser returns by navigation params in `pick` mode and saves itself in `edit` mode | One component serves creation, direct choice and later change |
| U-07 | Counting is shown as "Comptage rapide / complet" with the official word in brackets | The observer meets "plafonné" in the documents but must not confuse it with factor A's score cap (pitfall 1) |
