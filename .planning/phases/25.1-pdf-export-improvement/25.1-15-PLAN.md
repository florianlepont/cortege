## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-25.1-04 | Information disclosure | network during export | medium | mitigate | Offline: no call (test spies `fetch`); online: two optional reads raced against 4 s and 8 s; the WebView is locked by the CSP |
| T-25.1-02 | Tampering | file path of the export | medium | mitigate | Target always `cacheDirectory + "exports/" + buildExportFileName(...)` (ASCII slug), delete-then-move |
| T-25.1-05 | Information disclosure | old PDFs left in the cache | low | mitigate | Files older than one hour removed at the next export; the cache directory is OS-reclaimable |
| T-25.1-08 | Denial of service | a hanging optional piece | medium | mitigate | Every optional piece bounded and caught separately; `Promise.all` over caught branches |
| T-25.1-06 | Information disclosure | the PDF leaving the phone | low | accept | Only through the share sheet the user opens; no automatic upload |
</threat_model>
