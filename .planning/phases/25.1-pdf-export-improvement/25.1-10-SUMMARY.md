## What was built

- `identity.ts`: `inseeFromParcelId` (14-character id, `2A`/`2B` kept, `97x` gives three characters, malformed gives null). The identity block lists site, parcels, commune and département (first parcel), year, version number, French long date, observer and coordinates when present, and the capped survey mode; missing year, version or date read `Non renseigné`. The method block: v3.2 shows the version label, the cas label with its caption and the cas-3 scale line when `usesCas3Scale`; v3.0 shows region and stage labels (own-key lookup, so `constructor` or `__proto__` read as unknown); untagged shows `legacyVersionLabel`. Two-column tables, `identity-` classes, every text through `escapeHtml`.
- `score-section.ts`: three tiles (total /50, stand /35, context /15) with the band named from `fr.surveyDetail.bands` (total uses the `stand` ids, as the screen does), a dot and a bar in the `bandTone` colour, no threshold in the file. Null or non-finite scores print `scores.notFilled` and no band. `countMissingFactors` counts A to J absent or "Not filled"; a draft with some missing prints `scores.provisional`. `score-` classes.
- `factor-chart-svg.ts`: `buildFactorChartSvg(entries, palette, { width, height })` returns one `role="img"` SVG with grid lines, ten bars (height by points over `MAX_FACTOR_POINTS`, colour by `factorTone`, full-height outline bar in `inkFaint` with `fill="none"` for a missing factor), value labels and letters, a 26-unit gap between G and H (10 elsewhere). Points are clamped to 0 to 5, non-numbers read as missing. `buildFactorChartBlock` wraps it under `chartHeading`. `chart-` classes.
- 66 new tests across the three files (escaping with script and attribute payloads, both methods, drafts, hostile region codes, NaN and out-of-range points, CSS hygiene).
