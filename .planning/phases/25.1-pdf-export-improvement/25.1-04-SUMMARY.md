# Phase 25.1 Plan 04: Factor details stored with the survey Summary

The survey now stores the details it only counted before: `B.strata`, `F.dmh_groups`, `H.evidence`, `I.types` and `J.types` (arrays of catalogue codes), written next to the scored values, with the CNPF typology per method in the catalogue and one pure module that reads and labels them.

## What was built

- Catalogue: 11/10 aquatic and 12/9 rocky habitat types (v3.2/v3.0), 15 dendromicrohabitat groups, 3 continuity sources, `chips.legacyCount`, `companionLabels`. The old illustrative I/J lists stay until plan 25.1-08.
- `factor-selections.ts` is pure (no `./constants` import). `selectionOptionsFor` appends a selected code of the other method with its own label (never filtered on a method switch); `readSelection` keeps known codes of either method, de-duplicated, null when the key is absent.
- `useSurveyForm`: a detail array is written only with its scored key (F groups and H evidence only when non-empty); the B, I and J selection sets the count to its length, typing a count resets the selection to null; a bare-count draft reopens with the count and `selected === null`. One `FactorField` per factor is kept (no change to `computeFactorProgress`).
- Tests: 17 in `factor-selections.test.ts`, 12 new in `useSurveyForm.test.ts` (including identical retained scores with and without the arrays for v3.0 and v3.2 cas 1, and selections kept across a method switch).
