## Problems and findings for the later plans

1. **iOS composite style fails in the snapshotter** because of the sprite 404. Plan 25.1-11 must read the style file, drop `sprite` and hand the JSON string to `createImage` (verified on iOS with the file style and on Android with JSON). Without it the iOS map never appears even with a pack.
2. **Android offline packs cannot be created** from the app's `file://` composite style (and not from inline JSON): the download never starts. This is in the app's offline-area feature, outside this phase, but it means that on Android the "pack" path of the PDF cannot occur today (only ambient cache and online). Flag to the owner; not fixed here.
3. **Android snapshot hangs when any source of the style has no tiles offline.** The 8 s timeout is the only protection and it works. After a timeout the SVG outline is used.
4. **iOS leaves each snapshot PNG in the Documents directory** (`mlrn-snapshot-<uuid>.png`, 0.1 to 2.5 MB each). `map-snapshot.ts` should delete the file after the JPEG is made (it does not today).
5. **iOS print ignores `column-count`** and does not draw 1 px filled divs. Builders must use two explicit columns (grid or tables) and borders or SVG for hairlines.
6. **Android `numberOfPages` is useless** (10, 1, 10 for a 7 page file); only compare it on iOS.
7. The online decision uses the plain Plan IGN style (coloured, no cadastre) while the offline one is the grey style with cadastre: the printed map looks different depending on connectivity. A choice for plan 25.1-11.
8. iOS draws "Source : IGN" itself into the snapshot; the printed caption "Fond de carte : IGN" would be a duplicate on iOS.
9. The scale-bar label sits on its line in the spike; layout detail for plan 25.1-11.
10. Cold first export is slow on iOS (6 to 19 s in the sim, mostly photo manipulator and print warm-up). To check on the phone; a progress indication is already planned.
11. Not measured: iOS offline snapshot, real-phone times, the DebugTab buttons by tapping, the share sheet. A second AVD was needed because the existing `Pixel_8` holds an app signed with another key (see below).
