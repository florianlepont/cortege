## What was built

- `buildSurveyExportHtml(data)` returns `{ html, pageCount }`. Blocks are collected in the sheet order (identity, identity-method, score summary, chart, factor heading stand, A to G, heading context, H to J, map, trend, photo pages), paginated with the chrome (site, logo, `isDraft`, long French generation date) and wrapped in `div.pdf-root`.
- 27 end-to-end tests: head, CSP before content, one style element with every section's CSS and one `@font-face` per font, "Page i / N" footers for every page, section order (worst case), factors A to J in order, v3.2 (cas, scale lines) and v3.0 (region, stage, scale lines) contexts, draft (banner and watermark on every page, missing factors marked, provisional score) against submitted (none of it), hostile site name, observer and parcel ids escaped, no `<script`, `http:`, `https:`, `file:`, em dash or event-handler attribute in any of the four documents, font warm-up on page 1 when enabled.
- `survey-export-fixtures.ts`: v3.2 submitted (cas 1, one parcel, observer, GPS), v3.0 legacy draft (two factors missing, nothing optional), worst case (cas 4, all 34 genera, three parcels on a basemap, six history points across two methods, 24 photos).
- `buildExportFileName`: NFD, accents stripped, non-alphanumeric runs become one dash, 40 character cap, `releve` fallback, year from `observationYear` else the survey date else omitted, `-brouillon` for a draft; always `^[A-Za-z0-9-]+\.pdf$`. 11 tests including traversal strings.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `xmlns` url removed from the factor chart SVG.**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (the D-08 "no `http`" assertion on the whole document)
- **Issue:** `factor-chart-svg.ts` (plan 10) printed `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg"`, so the document held an `http:` string. Inline SVG in HTML does not need it (plan 12 already left it out of the trend SVG).
- **Fix:** removed the attribute and added a chart test that the SVG holds no `http`.
- **Files modified:** `mobile/src/app/survey-pdf/factor-chart-svg.ts`, `factor-chart-svg.test.ts`
- **Commit:** 08d408cc

**2. [Process] Task 1 test and implementation share one commit.** The assembler was written and its output inspected before the test file, so there is no separate RED commit for build-html (Task 2 has RED and GREEN commits).

## Notes for the next plans

- **Chart on page 1 is not guaranteed.** The score summary always shares page 1 with identity and method, but with a full identity (parcel, observer, GPS) the estimated heights (244 + 105 + 146 + 204 = 699) exceed the 681 capacity of a page (92 % of 740), so the factor chart opens page 2. A light sheet (the v3.0 draft fixture) keeps the chart on page 1. The estimates are untested on a device: plan 17 should compare a rendered first page and tune the identity or chart constants (or `PAGE_FILL_FACTOR`) if the pages come out loose.
- Typical page counts: v3.2 submitted 5 pages, v3.0 draft 4, worst case 10.
- The fixtures import `@cortege/ibp-domain` genus codes and `ownItem`; they live in `mobile/test/` and nothing under `src` imports them.
- `buildSurveyExportHtml` needs a `react-native` mock in Jest (palette goes through `theme.ts`), as `palette.test.ts` does.
- The runner (plan 15) must pass `buildExportFileName` an input from `SurveyExportData` (`siteName`, `observationYear`, `dateIso`, `isDraft`) and keep the target directory fixed; the name alone cannot escape it.
