### Calibration fixes made (with tests)

The page counts matched and no card was cut, but the heights of page 1 were over-estimated, so the
factor chart of an ordinary v3.2 survey (identity with observer and coordinates) moved to page 2 and
page 1 stayed 40 percent empty on both platforms, although the owner's check step 2 expects the chart
on page 1. Measured on the printed PDFs (`pdftotext -bbox` heading positions): identity card 245.3
(estimate 244), method card 93.4 (estimate 105), score card 136 (estimate 146), chart 205.8
(estimate 204).

- `identity.ts`: `VALUE_CHARS_PER_LINE` 62 to 80 (the 78 character cas 1 line and the 64 character
  site name each print on one line; about 3.7 pt per character).
- `score-section.ts`: `TILE_HEIGHT` 92 to 84 (printed 82).
- Result: identity 244 + method 92 + scores 138 + chart 204 = 678 of the 681 capacity; printed 680
  of the 740 of the body. The chart now sits on page 1 on both platforms for the v3.2 and the worst
  case, a draft still fits (641 of 658). 2 new tests (`identity.test.ts`, `build-html.test.ts`).
- `PAGE_FILL_FACTOR` (0.92) and the factor card constants were left alone: C to F print 107.6 against
  117 estimated, A prints 291 against 310 (34 genera), B prints 110 against 107 (3 under, covered by
  the 59 units of margin). Every worst-case card fits with room.
