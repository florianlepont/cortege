# Phase 25.1 Plan 08: Factor input UI for the stored selections Summary

The observer can now tick and re-see the strata (B), the CNPF aquatic and rocky habitat types of the survey's method (I, J), the 15 dendromicrohabitat groups (F) and the 3 continuity sources (H), all through chips controlled by the selection that plan 25.1-04 stores.

## What was built

- `FactorChipsInput` has no internal state: it ticks `selected`, reports the next list in option order through `onSelectionChange`, and with `selected === null` shows no ticked chip plus the `legacyCount` line (a draft saved before the phase). `filled` forces the filled shell state (a recorded zero for I or J).
- `FactorDetailScreen`: `fieldVariantsFor(factor, methodVersion)` replaces the static table. B, I and J read their options from `selectionOptionsFor` (v3.2: 11 I and 12 J types; v3.0 or untagged: 10 and 9; a selected code of the other method stays offered and ticked). F renders the 15 group chips under trees per hectare, H the 3 source chips under the class segments, both labelled by `companionLabels`, bound to `field.selection`, optional, adding no `FactorField` (completeness and scores unchanged).
- The old `aquaticHabitatOptions` and `rockyHabitatOptions` lists are deleted from the catalogue; nothing else read them.
- Tests: 9 in `FactorChipsInput.test.tsx` (rewritten), 10 new cases in `FactorDetailScreen.test.tsx`.
