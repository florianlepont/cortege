<objective>
Build the trend card of the parcel history page: `TrendCurve` (react-native-svg curve with the reveal animation) and `TrendCard` (forest card with the trend title, the curve and the mixed-method notice).

Purpose: D-06 and D-10 (sketch 012 winner B+C), following the UI-SPEC "4a. Trend card", "4b. Curve geometry" and Motion contract, with the geometry from plan 24-04 and the texts from plan 24-05.
Output: two presentational components with tests. They take plain data (`TrendSummary`, `TrendInputPoint[]`); the page assembly is plan 24-10.

Reveal technique (RESEARCH R6, assumption A1; Claude's discretion in CONTEXT): the UI-SPEC prefers a clip-rect wipe (`ClipPath` + animated `Rect` width), which has no precedent in this repo; the proven fallback is `strokeDashoffset` on the run paths (precedent `ui/ScoreRing.tsx`) with the points and labels fading in. Both are implemented behind one exported switch `TREND_REVEAL_MODE` (precedent: `NUMERAL_RENDER_MODE`, Phase 23-04), and Task 1 starts with a time-boxed simulator spike that sets the default. If the spike cannot run or is inconclusive, the default is `"dash"` (proven on iOS and Android). The owner phone check (plan 24-12) confirms the reveal on the device.
</objective>
