# Phase 01.8 Plan 13: Survey form screens for the method version Summary

The survey wizard now asks which IBP method the survey follows (v3.2 preselected, v3.0 available) on its identity step, shows the scoring context of that method on the parcels step (cas 1–4 with captions and the "Échelle du cas 3 pour A et G" switch for v3.2; region and stage for v3.0 and untagged drafts), picks the factor help for the survey's method, and shows totals out of 50.

## What changed

**Identity step**
- `MethodVersionPicker` (new) renders at the top of `SiteSection`: title `fr.ibpMethod.versionTitle`, two `AppChoiceChip`s (`versions[v3.2]`, `versions[v3.0]`), the subtitle `versionHint` (or `versionLockedHint` when locked, with the chips non-pressable). A `null` version selects the v3.0 chip and shows `legacyVersionLabel`.
- The `SurveyFormMethod` type (`version`, `cas`, `cas3Scale`, `locked`, `setVersion`, `setCas`, `setCas3Scale`) is exported from the picker file; `SurveyFormRoute` builds it with `useMemo` from `state.ibpMethodVersion/ibpCas/ibpCas3Scale` and the three form setters.

**Parcels step**
- `RegionVegetationSection.tsx` was renamed with `git mv` to `ScoringContextSection.tsx` (git records it as delete + add because the content changed by more than half).
- v3.2: `fr.ibpMethod.casTitle`, four `WizardChip`s (`casLabels`) each beside its `casCaptions` text, a "Cas à choisir" line when no cas is set, and the cas-3 switch (label + hint).
- v3.0 and untagged: the former region and stage chips, unchanged.
- `scoringContextPills` feeds the hero: "Cas 1" + "IBP v3.2 (2026)" for a v3.2 survey ("Cas à choisir" without a cas), region + stage labels otherwise. `buildHeroCopy` now takes `contextPills` instead of `regionLabel`/`vegetationLabel`.

**Factors**
- `FactorDetailScreen` takes `methodVersion` and uses `helpForMethod(methodVersion)` for the hero help and the capture hints; `FactorDetailRoute` passes `state.ibpMethodVersion`. A renders the count and native-cover fields the hook gives it, B only strata.
- `FactorsList` shows `fr.surveyForm.factors.scoreTotal` ("12 / 50"); the header pill `ibpTotal` reads "IBP 12 / 50". The `/10 facteurs` factor-count strings are unchanged.
- `FACTOR_ORDER` in `components.tsx` is `[...FACTOR_KEYS]` from the package (no factor-key literal left).

**Catalogue (`survey-form.ts`)**
- `header.ibpTotal` → `IBP {total} / 50`, new `factors.scoreTotal`, new `scoringContext.casSubtitle` and `scoringContext.casMissing`; `header.parcelsBody` mentions the cas or, in v3.0, region and stage.
