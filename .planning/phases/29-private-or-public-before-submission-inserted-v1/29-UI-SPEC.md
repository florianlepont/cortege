# Phase 29 - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 29 (Private or Public Before Submission). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, to be verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Locked upstream: 29-CONTEXT D-02 (two labels, "public" never shown), D-04 (selector in the submission step, "Partagé avec l'association" pre-selected), D-05 (draft stores the choice), D-07 (frozen after submit), D-15 (read-only line on the owner's survey detail). The seam (a native form sheet reached from "Terminer le relevé") comes from 29-RESEARCH section M2.
> Everything the context did not fix is decided here with a recommended default and marked "(recommended, to confirm at the owner UI gate)". They are collected in "Open points for the owner" at the end.
> Translation of the template for this stack: "design system" = `mobile/src/app/brand-tokens.ts` + `theme.ts` + `visual-tokens.ts` + `theme-visual.ts`; "component library" = `mobile/src/ui/`; "breakpoints" = none (phone portrait, Dynamic Type); shadcn does not apply (no `components.json`).
> No new colour, no new font file, no new runtime dependency, no new animation. The phase reuses existing tokens and primitives and defines none of its own.

## Spacing Scale

`brandSpacing4` (strict 4-grid). The legacy `brandSpacing` (6/10/22/28) is not used in new code, except `brandSpacing.sm` (8) already used by the card list.

| Token | Value | Usage in this phase |
|-------|-------|---------------------|
| xxs | 2 | radio top offset inside a card (inherited from the card) |
| xs | 4 | gap title to caption inside a card; gap between the status line and the visibility line on the summary |
| sm | 8 | gap between the two cards; gap between the confirm and cancel buttons; gap glyph to text in the visibility line |
| smd | 12 | gap radio to copy inside a card; gap between footnote and notice |
| md | 16 | sheet top padding; gap title to cards; gap cards to footnote |
| 20 | 20 | sheet horizontal padding (multiple of 4; the same value as `FactorHelpSheet`) |
| lg | 24 | gap footnote/notice to the button group; sheet bottom padding on top of the safe-area inset |

Exceptions (inherited, not new): the radio card keeps `CasPicker`'s padding 14, radio 22 pt and `brandRadius.field`, so the cas cards and the visibility cards read the same. Nothing new is off the 4-grid. Touch targets: radio cards at least 64 high (CasPicker `minHeight`), confirm 50 (`GlassButton size="lg"`), cancel 44 (`size="md"`, never below `brandInteraction.hitTarget.min`).

## Typography

Four sizes (24, 14, 13, 12) and two weights (Regular 400, SemiBold 600) on every new text. Button labels are drawn by `GlassButton` (16, its own contract, not restyled).

| Role | Size / line | Weight | Font (token) | Used for |
|------|-------------|--------|--------------|----------|
| Heading | 24 / 28 | SemiBold 600, tracking -0.6 | `Sora-SemiBold` (`brandTypography.screenTitle`) | sheet title "Qui peut voir ce relevé ?" |
| Card title | 14 / 20 | SemiBold 600 | `Jost-SemiBold` (`brandTypography.sectionBody` with `fontFamily: brandTypography.meta.fontFamily`, exactly as `CasPicker`) | "Partagé avec l'association", "Privé" |
| Footnote | 13 / 18 | Regular 400 | `Jost-Regular` (`brandTypeScale.footnote` + `brandDefaultFontFamily`) | frozen-after-submit footnote; the visibility line on the summary; notice text through `AppNotice` |
| Caption | 12 / 16 | SemiBold 600 | `Jost-SemiBold` (`brandTypography.meta`) | the one-line explanation under each card title |

Rules:
- Every text uses `AppText`, so Dynamic Type caps apply (title 1.5, body 1.6, meta 1.6, button 1.4). Cards grow; nothing is truncated (no `numberOfLines` on titles, captions, footnote or the visibility line).
- The sheet title uses the standard page-title role (24 / 28). `FactorHelpSheet` keeps its older 22 title; harmonising it is out of scope (open point 9).
- French copy has no em dash (U+2014) anywhere; no "->" ASCII arrow; typographic apostrophe is the straight `'` used by the rest of the catalogue.

## Surface 1 - The finish sheet ("Qui peut voir ce relevé ?")

Files: new route `surveyFinish` (`{ surveyId: string }`), `FinishSheetRoute` (`navigation/routes/`), `FinishSheet` (`screens/`), `useFinishSheet` (`screens/finish-sheet/`, orchestration), `VisibilityPicker` and `RadioCard` (`ui/`).

### Opening

| Entry | Behaviour |
|-------|-----------|
| Summary `FinishBar`, kind `ready` | `onFinish` becomes `navigation.navigate("surveyFinish", { surveyId })` (was `onSubmitSurvey(id)`). Button label, `GlassButton`, a11y label unchanged ("Terminer le relevé", `finishSurvey(name)`). Kinds `next` and `disabled` are unchanged |
| Factor pager pill "Terminer" | `usePagerFinish` navigates to the same route instead of calling `flushDraft()` then `submitSurvey`; the flush moves into the sheet's confirm (29-RESEARCH M2). The pager's `onFinished` (`popTo("surveyDetail")`, keyed on status `submitted`) is unchanged |
| Initial selection | the stored visibility of the survey from the surveys context: `"private"` shows "Privé", anything else shows "Partagé avec l'association" (a new draft is stored as shared, D-04; an unknown value fails closed in storage, not here). Reopening after a cancel or an offline confirm shows the stored value |

### Layout (top to bottom, one `ScrollView`, `contentInsetAdjustmentBehavior="never"`, background canvas)

| # | Block | Contract |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | Title | `Text` Heading, `accessibilityRole="header"`: "Qui peut voir ce relevé ?". No close glyph (cancel is a button below; iOS also has the grabber) |
| 2 | `VisibilityPicker` | container `accessibilityRole="radiogroup"` and `accessibilityLabel` "Visibilité du relevé"; two `RadioCard`s, gap 8, order: "Partagé avec l'association" first (the pre-selected default), "Privé" second (recommended, to confirm at the owner UI gate) |
| 3 | Footnote | Footnote role, `textSecondary`: "Ce choix ne pourra plus être modifié une fois le relevé terminé." Always visible |
| 4 | Notice slot | at most one `AppNotice` (offline, save error or frozen; see states), gap 12 under the footnote |
| 5 | Button group | gap 24 above, gap 8 between: confirm `GlassButton variant="primary" size="lg"` full width, then cancel `GlassButton variant="secondary" size="md"` "Annuler" full width |

Padding: horizontal 20, top 16, bottom 24 plus the bottom safe-area inset (`useSafeAreaInsets().bottom`); on Android the top padding is 16 plus the top inset (the modal has no header, it sits under the status bar). Vertical gap between blocks 1, 2 and 3 is 16.

### Radio card (`RadioCard`, extracted from `CasPicker`, `VisibilityPicker` passes its copy)

| Part | Contract |
|------|----------|
| Frame | `AppPressable`, row, `alignItems: flex-start`, gap 12, `minHeight` 64, padding 14, `borderRadius: brandRadius.field`, border 1 `colors.divider`, fill `surfaceElevated`. Selected: border 2 `ctaPrimary` |
| Radio | 22 pt circle, ring 2 `textSecondary`; selected: ring and fill `ctaPrimary` with `checkmark-outline` 14 in `onCtaPrimary` |
| Copy | title (Card title role) with a leading 18 pt glyph in the title row (gap 8: `people-outline` for shared, `lock-closed-outline` for private), then the caption (Caption role) under it, gap 2 (`xxs`). The glyph is decorative (`accessible={false}`, no label) |
| Press | the standard `AppPressable` press response; no custom animation |
| testIDs | `visibility-option-shared`, `visibility-option-private` (the wire values stay out of the ids) |

### States

| State | What the sheet shows |
|-------|----------------------|
| Default | stored choice selected, confirm "Terminer le relevé" enabled, "Annuler" enabled, no notice |
| Selection changed | tap on the other card: it becomes selected immediately (instant, no animation), `feedback.selection()` fires once; a tap on the already selected card does nothing and fires nothing. Nothing is stored until the confirm: cancel, swipe down or Android back discard the change |
| Pressed | `AppPressable` default press scale and opacity (`brandInteraction.pressedScale` 0.97, `pressedOpacity` 0.9) |
| Offline (`useIsOffline()`) | `AppNotice tone="warning" icon="cloud-offline-outline"` with the offline message; confirm label becomes "Enregistrer le choix" (the finish cannot run offline, so the button must not say "Terminer"); the tap stores the choice on the draft (D-05) and closes the sheet, and the existing status message `submitPostponed` explains the rest |
| Submitting (from the confirm tap until the finish returns) | both cards locked (`disabled`, `accessibilityState.disabled`, NOT dimmed, so the observer still sees what is being sent); confirm `loading` (spinner, keeps the forest look, ignores presses); "Annuler" `disabled` (token dim 0.4); the iOS swipe-down is off (`gestureEnabled: false` set through `navigation.setOptions` while submitting) and Android back is ignored (`usePreventRemove`); a polite announcement "Envoi du relevé en cours" |
| Success | the stored status becomes `submitted`: the sheet closes itself (`goBack()` only if still mounted; in the pager path `popTo("surveyDetail")` has already removed it). The success haptic and the score-card pulse come from `useSubmitSuccessPulse` unchanged |
| Finish returned without submitting | any other outcome of `finishSurvey` (offline postponed, name or readiness check failed, login required, sync round failed, submit rejected): the sheet closes and the existing status message (`surveyOps.submit*`) is shown by the summary or the pager as today. The choice is already stored on the draft. If the executor finds that a path does not surface the message, the sheet shows it in an `AppNotice` and stays open instead (contingency, not the default) |
| Save error (the local write of the choice threw) | `AppNotice tone="danger" icon="alert-circle-outline"`: "Impossible d'enregistrer votre choix sur cet appareil. Réessayez." The sheet stays open, the cards and buttons are live again, `feedback.notify.error()` fires once, the finish is NOT attempted |
| Frozen (the sheet is opened on a survey already `submitted`, or the server refused the visibility with `survey_submitted_read_only` / `submitted_read_only_fields`) | cards locked, the selected card shows the stored value, the footnote is replaced by `AppNotice tone="info" icon="lock-closed-outline"`: "Ce relevé est déjà terminé : sa visibilité ne peut plus être modifiée."; only one button remains, "Fermer" (secondary glass); no confirm. Reached only by a race, never from the normal flow |
| Long names, large Dynamic Type | cards grow in height, the `ScrollView` scrolls, confirm stays the last item and reachable; the sheet expands to its large detent on scroll |

### Confirm sequence (what the tap does, in order)

1. busy on; 2. `flushDraft()` when a pager edit is active (resolves `true` when nothing is being edited); 3. if the chosen value differs from the stored one, a queue-only visibility write (`updateSurveyVisibility(apiUrl, "", id, choice)`, no sync, no status message; skipped when equal); 4. `actions.submitSurvey(id)` (drains the queue, then submits; one finish at a time per survey is already guaranteed by `finishingSurveyIds`); 5. outcome as in the table above; busy off. A thrown error in step 2 or 3 is the "Save error" state.

## Surface 2 - Read-only visibility line on the owner's survey detail

Files: `SummaryHeader.tsx` (new optional `visibilityLine` prop, 142 lines before), new pure `screens/survey-detail/visibility-line.ts` (value to icon, text and a11y label, unit tested), `SurveyDetailScreen.tsx` (passes the prop, at most 10 added lines), catalogue.

Decision (recommended, to confirm at the owner UI gate): a line under the status line of the title block, not a fifth row of the grouped list and not a header chip. Reasons: the grouped-list value is a single fixed-width line and "Partagé avec l'association" would clip at large Dynamic Type; the status line is where the app already says where the survey stands; the line wraps naturally.

| Property | Contract |
|----------|----------|
| Shown for | the owner's own survey summary only, draft and finished. Never on sub-pages, never on the community page (`CommunitySurveyScreen`), never in the Explorer sheet, search results, Accueil cards or the PDF (D-16, D-17) |
| Position | last child of `titleBlock`, directly under the status line, gap 4 |
| Layout | row: outline glyph 14 (`lock-closed-outline` or `people-outline`, colour `textSecondary`) then text, gap 8, Footnote role, wraps to as many lines as needed, no truncation |
| Not pressable | no handler, no chevron, no ripple. Visibility cannot change after submit, and on a draft it is chosen at the finish step; the line must not invite a tap |
| Finished survey | text: "Partagé avec l'association" or "Privé" (the same two labels as the sheet) |
| Draft | text: "Sera partagé avec l'association (choix à la fin)" or "Restera privé (choix à la fin)" (recommended, to confirm; hide the line on drafts is the alternative) |
| a11y | one element, `accessible`, `accessibilityRole="text"`, label: finished "Visibilité : partagé avec l'association. Ne peut plus être modifiée." / "Visibilité : privé. Ne peut plus être modifiée."; draft "Visibilité : sera partagé avec l'association, choix à la fin." / "Visibilité : restera privé, choix à la fin." (glyph hidden from the tree) |
| Offline | works offline: the value is the local row's `visibility` |
| After the 024 backfill | an old submitted survey reads "Partagé avec l'association" once the owner's phone has pulled the `backfilled` event (29-RESEARCH Migration) |

### List rows (Mes Relevés, Accueil, search "Mes relevés")

No badge, no new glyph, no new text (recommended, to confirm at the owner UI gate, open point 5). Reasons: the list is about progress and score; private is a deliberate, uncommon choice that is visible one tap away; a lock on some rows only adds noise and a second place to keep in sync with the detail. Alternative if the owner wants it: a 14 pt `lock-closed-outline` in `textSecondary` after the status text of private rows only, with a11y suffix ", privé". The `visibilityFilter` state/filter has no UI home and is removed (CONTEXT D-15, 29-RESEARCH Q5): not a design element.

## Platform behaviour (iOS native form sheet versus Android)

New options helper `finishSheetScreenOptions(theme)` in `stack-options.ts`, a sibling of `factorHelpScreenOptions`.

| | iOS | Android and JS tabs |
|---|-----|---------------------|
| Presentation | `formSheet` (system grabber, system corner radius) | `modal` (full screen slides up over the pager or the summary) |
| Detents | `sheetAllowedDetents: [0.65, 1]` (recommended: the default text size shows both cards, footnote and both buttons without scrolling on a 6.1 inch phone; verify on the owner iPhone and adjust the fraction only), `sheetGrabberVisible: true`; the sheet expands to 1 when the content is scrolled to the edge | n/a (full screen) |

| Background | `contentStyle: { backgroundColor: semanticColors.backgroundCanvas }` | same |

| Tab bar | the sheet is presented above the tab bar on both platforms; nothing to hide (`shouldHideTabBar` unchanged) | same |

Device checks (owner workflow, not automatable): both entry points open the same sheet; the title is not drawn under scrolled content; the bottom padding clears the home indicator; Android top padding clears the status bar; the two glass buttons stacked look right; light and dark.

## Explicitly NOT designed in this phase

- Any change of Explorer, map, parcel panel, community survey page or global search UI (they only receive what the server returns, D-16).
- A "public", "private" or lock badge on community surveys or on other members' results (the server hides private surveys entirely, D-11; there is nothing to badge).
- A visibility control after submit, a menu entry to change it, or any "make public later" path (deferred; read-only).
- Sharing with named members or groups, a "shared with a structure" level.
- A visibility row in the PDF export (D-17) or in the survey journal.
- A visibility filter on Mes Relevés (`visibilityFilter` is removed, not given a UI).
- A confirmation alert on top of the sheet.
- A new onboarding or explanatory screen about privacy; the two captions and the footnote are the whole explanation.
- Changing the existing sync error strings or the `surveyOps.submit*` messages.
