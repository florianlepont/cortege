<objective>
Build the submission step that does not exist today: a native form sheet `surveyFinish` that shows the visibility choice (pre-selected from the stored value), states that it freezes at submit, and whose confirm button stores the choice and runs the existing finish (D-04, D-05, D-07). This plan registers the route; plan 29-08 points both "Terminer le relevé" entries at it.

Purpose: `REQ-C-privacy-choice` sub-claim C1 (choose before submitting) and the client side of C2 and C3; Surface 1 of 29-UI-SPEC.md.
Output: a pure outcome mapper, the orchestration hook, the sheet screen and its outcome notice, the route, its options and registration, with tests.

Deviations from the UI-SPEC defaults, recorded for the owner (both use the UI-SPEC's own contingency clauses):
1. "Finish returned without submitting" while online: the sheet stays open and shows the status message of that moment in a notice, instead of closing. Reason found in the code: the summary never displays status messages (only Paramètres reads the status line, see `PictureStatusAlert.tsx` OA-76), so closing would make a failed finish silent from the summary. Offline the sheet closes, because the offline notice shown before the tap already says the choice is stored and the survey can be finished once back online.
2. The Android back press is blocked with `BackHandler` while submitting, not `usePreventRemove`: `usePreventRemove` would also block the pager's programmatic return to the summary when the submit succeeds.
</objective>
