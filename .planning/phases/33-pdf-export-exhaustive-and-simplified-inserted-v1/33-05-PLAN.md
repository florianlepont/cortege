<objective>
Build Surface A of the UI-SPEC: the export choice sheet's content, its picker, its state hook and its own catalogue module (D-05, D-09, D-14, D-15, D-18). The route that presents it as a native form sheet (iOS) or a modal (Android), and the summary screen that runs the export, come in plans 33-09 and 33-12.

Purpose: the member chooses at export time between the complete PDF and the one-page summary, with the approximate page count on the "Complet" card, and is told why the summary is unavailable on a draft. A new catalogue module keeps `survey-detail.ts` (edited by phases 31 and 32) untouched.
Output: `fr.surveyExportChoice` registered in `fr`, `useExportChoice`, `ExportModePicker`, `ExportChoiceSheet`, its styles, and tests.

Constraints (CLAUDE.md, UI-SPEC A.6): colours only through `useBrandTheme()`; no user-facing literal outside `src/i18n`; every style key read; Ionicons outline glyphs only; haptics only through `ui/feedback.ts`; no animation beyond the existing `AppPressable` press; `RadioCard`, `VisibilityPicker` and `FinishSheet` are not changed; each file well under 400 lines; no U+2014. "Partager" stays the header label (D-15): the sheet title makes the PDF explicit. A submitted survey not yet synced counts as submitted (D-18): the caller passes `isDraft` from `status !== "submitted"`.
