<objective>
Build the cover page of the complete PDF (D-11, UI-SPEC B.4) as one pure block with `pageStyle: "cover"`: the half-bleed forest panel (contours, logo tile, eyebrow, title, date, total score with its band pill, provisional line) and the light part (sub-score tiles with rings, six identity facts).

Purpose: D-02 asks for a cover, large numerals and charter accents; research lever L5 moves the score card's content onto the cover so the cover costs no extra page. Plan 33-13 registers the section first in the complete mode and removes the old score card.
Output: `cover-section.ts` and its tests.

Constraints: pure builder (no react-native, no storage, no time read); colours only from `PdfPalette`; fonts from `PDF_FONT_FAMILIES`; texts only from `fr.surveyExport`; every position on the 4-grid as UI-SPEC B.4 gives them; numbers through `svgNumber` and `formatDecimal`; `escapeHtml` on every survey text; class prefix `cover-`. This plan does not touch `sections.ts` or `build-html.test.ts` (plan 33-13 owns them).
