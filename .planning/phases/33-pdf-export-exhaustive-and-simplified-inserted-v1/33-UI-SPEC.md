> Visual and interaction contract for the two design surfaces of the phase: (A) the export choice sheet `surveyExportChoice`, and (B) the printed documents (the complete PDF with its cover, and the one-page summary). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Sources: 33-CONTEXT.md (D-01..D-18, locked; nothing was re-asked), 33-RESEARCH.md (sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and Open Questions), CLAUDE.md, charter section 13 and `direction-visuelle-12-2.md`, 27-UI-SPEC.md (format), and the code: `ui/RadioCard.tsx`, `ui/VisibilityPicker.tsx`, `screens/FinishSheet.tsx`, `screens/finish-sheet/finish-sheet.styles.ts`, `navigation/stacks/stack-options.ts`, `app/survey-pdf/*` (palette, styles, paginate, score, chart, trend, map, identity, factor, photos), `app/contour-paths.ts`, `app/visual-tokens.ts`, `app/theme-visual.ts`.

## Spacing Scale

Surface A uses `brandSpacing4` only (4-grid). Surface B uses design units; every NEW value is a multiple of 4. The only non-multiples that remain are the legacy values listed under Exceptions, each tied to a device measurement.

| Token | Value | Usage (Surface A, pt) | Usage (Surface B, units) |
|-------|-------|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| xs | 4 | Gap inside a card between title and caption (RadioCard `xxs` 2 stays as is) | Accent bar width of a section title, band dot margin, pill vertical padding |
| sm | 8 | Gap between the two buttons; gap icon to title in a card | Logo tile inset, band dot size, padding-left of a section title, card inner tile radius, cover fact top padding |
| smd | 12 | Gap between the two radio cards; gap above the draft sentence | Gap between summary zones; card radius; pill horizontal padding |
| md | 16 | Title to picker; sheet top padding | Gap between the two cover tiles; summary Z2 column gap; cover fact bottom padding; tile inner padding |
| lg | 24 | Picker/sentence to buttons; sheet bottom padding (+ safe area) | Cover column gap of the facts grid; logo tile radius |
| 28 | 28 | not used | Cover gap panel to tiles and tiles to facts |
| xl | 32 | not used | not used |

Fixed sizes (Surface A):
- Sheet horizontal padding 20 (as `finish-sheet.styles.ts`, `SHEET_PADDING_X`).
- `RadioCard` keeps its own metrics: minHeight 64, padding 14, radius `brandRadius.field` 18, 22 pt radio, 18 pt glyph. No change to the shared component.
- Buttons: `GlassButton` `size="lg"` primary (the big CTA), `size="md"` secondary. Touch targets are at least 44 pt everywhere.

Cover and summary row arithmetic (all multiples of 4): a cover fact cell is 8 (top padding) + 12 (caption line) + 20 (value line) + 16 (bottom padding) = 56, plus the 1-unit top hairline; three rows = 168.

Exceptions:
- Accepted deviation (legacy calibration, Surface A): `RadioCard` inner padding 14 and radius 18 are the shared component's existing values (also used by `CasPicker` and `VisibilityPicker`); the sheet padding 20 is the finish sheet's.
- Accepted deviation (legacy calibration, Surface B): the existing print values 3 (row padding), 6 (row and card paddings), 10 (card padding, card margin, factor-card padding), 14 (`factor-group` padding, footer bottom, header top is 16), 18 (draft banner strip) and the chrome geometry 16/34/60/22/40 are kept untouched because the block-height estimates in `identity.ts`, `score-section.ts`, `factor-section.ts` and `paginate.ts` are calibrated on them by the 25.1-07 and 25.1-17 device measurements (33-RESEARCH pitfall 1). They are not copied into any new element.
- 1-unit hairlines (borders, rules) are not spacing.
- The new factor-card left border is 4 with a left padding of 8 (12 in total instead of 11 today); the content width shrinks by 1 unit, so `OBS_WIDTH` 313 becomes 312 and is retuned with the other estimates (B.9).
