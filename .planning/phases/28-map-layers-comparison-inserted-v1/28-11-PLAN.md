<objective>
Build the pieces that make layers tell the truth on the map: which layers are usable at the view centre offline, the ordered notices (offline, coverage, error, over-zoom, hidden surveys), the pill column that shows them, and a small timer hook for the transient pills.

Purpose: success criterion 4 (layers behave as decided with and without a network, visibly) and the over-zoom decision (CONTEXT: scaled tiles accepted, the UI must handle over-zoom without a blank map); criterion 3 (nothing hidden without saying why: the hidden-surveys pill). Output: `map-notices.ts`, `useLayerAvailability.ts`, `useTransientFlag.ts`, the glyph option of `MapInfoPill`, and `MapNoticeStack.tsx`, with tests.
