## Scope of the contract

One screen (Explorer, `PublicMapScreen`) plus one row in Paramètres. **The Explorer's design is kept**: the top-right capsule with the globe (basemap) button and the download button together, and the locate arrow at the bottom right, unchanged in look and place. Nothing is added to the screen at rest except one round overlay button under the capsule. Everything else appears on demand.

| Surface | What this contract defines |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Capsule (globe + download) and locate arrow | Unchanged shapes; the globe now enters the basemap swipe mode (sections 1 and 3) |
| Overlay round button and its bubble | Types de forêt switch with legend, Parcelles Toutes / Avec IBP / Aucune (section 2) |
| Basemap swipe mode | Indicator pill, "Terminé", transient hint, wipe gesture, freezing, exits (section 3, 3b offline) |
| Comparison by release in the middle | States, side wipes, line, edge exit, the centred "Quitter la comparaison" pill, markers hidden (section 4) |
| Curtain look | Line, knob, side tags (section 5) |
| Compact bottom card and chip | Estimate, "Télécharger", chip-only progress, failed and partial results (section 6) |
| Paramètres > Cartes | "Couches hors ligne" row and its action sheet (section 7) |
| Top notices column, legend and attribution, states | Over-zoom, coverage, offline, error, sources (sections 8 to 10) |

**No "Couches" pill, no big panel, no Comparer button, no basemap bottom card, no thumbnails chooser, no sheet-based download view.**

Base keys: `BasemapKey = "map" | "satellite"` stays for the picker, the PDF and the saved preference. The swipe layers use `MapLayerKey = "map" | "satellite" | "etat-major" | "carte-1950" | "photos-1950"` for bases and `"types-foret"` for the overlay. Order of the basemap cycle (locked): **Plan, Satellite, Carte d'état-major, Carte de 1950, Photos aériennes (1950-1965)**, wrapping around.

Study state is `{ base, overlay, parcels, compare: { sideA, sideB, x } | null, swipeMode }` with `parcels: "all" | "withIbp" | "none"`, held in `useMapLayersState`. **Persistence:** a **Plan or Satellite** chosen by the swipe writes the saved basemap preference (`map_basemap`), as the old switch did. The **old bases**, the overlay, Parcelles and the comparison are **session-only**: reset on cold start, kept while the app lives and across tab changes (tabs stay mounted). "Default" means: base = the saved preference, overlay off, Parcelles = Toutes, no comparison.

## Color

All values come from the theme; light and dark follow the system only. Basemaps are not scheme aware (they stay light in dark mode) except the recoloured Plan, so every control over a map takes a dense surface.

| Role | Token | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | the map itself (raster basemap) | Not themed. Behind it `brandMapTokens.darkBasemapBackground` (`#0E1512`) as ground in dark |
| Secondary (30%) | `visual.mapControl.glass` (capsule, overlay button, indicator pill, "Terminé", "Quitter la comparaison", knob, tags, notice pills, chip); `visual.mapPanel` with the theme's text (overlay bubble, compact card) | Every control and card over the map |
| Accent (10%) | `visual.tab.dot` (moss) for the two dots; `visual.accentText` for links; native `Switch` on-track | Reserved list below |
| Destructive | not used | The failed download glyph uses `onSurface.danger`, glyph only |

Accent reserved for (exhaustive):
1. The 8 pt dot on the globe button (base differs from the saved one, or a comparison is on).
2. The 8 pt dot on the overlay button (Types de forêt on, or Parcelles other than Toutes).
3. The "Réessayer" links (notices and the failed card).
4. The leading tile glyph of the bubble row (`visual.glass.iconTint`).
5. The `Switch` on state.

(The active Parcelles segment uses the `AppChoiceChip` active style; chips are not accent, charter 13.4.)

Other colour rules:
- **Curtain line**: white core `visual.curtain.line`, edges `visual.curtain.edge` (graphite at about 45% alpha), same in both schemes, declared per scheme in `theme-visual.ts`. The swipe dots reuse them (white fill, 1 pt edge ring).
- **Hint scrim**: `visual.mapHint { fill, ink }`, graphite at 70% alpha or more with a white ink (the engine uses 55%; the planner raises it until white text holds 4.5:1 over the lightest basemap, tested). **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**
- Offline and error lines: `cloud-offline-outline` / `alert-circle-outline` (error in `onSurface.danger`), text in secondary colour; no red text, no tinted box.
- **Parcels and cadastre on the map (Parcelles control).** Same on the left and right map while comparing:

  | State | Cadastre lines (raster) | Parcel polygons (fill + outline) | Taps |
  |-------|-------------------------|----------------------------------|------|
  | Toutes (default) | Drawn from zoom 15, opacity 0.9 (0.7 on Carte d'état-major and Carte de 1950) | All the polygons the Explorer draws today, with today's tokens: score-band fill for a scored parcel, warm grey (`parcelUnscored`) for one without a usable score, neutral for the others | As today |
  | Avec IBP | **Not drawn** | Only the parcels with at least one IBP survey in the Explorer data (a `hasSurvey` feature property drives a layer filter): score-band fill for a scored one, warm grey for one without a usable total. Parcels without a survey are not drawn | On drawn polygons as today |
  | Aucune | **Not drawn** | **Not drawn** | Parcels are not tappable; markers and clusters still are |

  Toutes is today's rendering. A parcel focus coming from the search (Phase 25, selected terracotta) is **always drawn**, whatever the state, so a result never lands on an empty map.
- **Z-order** (bottom to top): base, Types de forêt (opacity 0.55), cadastre lines (Toutes only), parcel casing, parcel fill, parcel outline, markers (hidden while comparing). Green still means a high score only.
- **Parcels on the old bases.** The three old bases (Carte d'état-major, Carte de 1950, Photos 1950) look washed out under today's fill and a white casing. On them, in both schemes (they are light in dark mode too), the parcel **fill alpha is multiplied by `brandMapTokens.parcelFillOldBaseFactor` = 0.6** (a score-band fill drops from 0.55 to about 0.33; outlines keep width and colour). `brandMapTokens.parcelCasing` (white at about 85% alpha) is drawn under `parcels-outline` with width `strokeWidth + 2`, **only** on the old bases and when Types de forêt is on, with the same 0.6 factor on the old bases. Plan and Satellite without the overlay keep today's exact rendering, in both schemes. **[discretion, owner reviews on device: one number to tune]**
- **Dark mode of the rasters:** Plan keeps `darkenPlanIgnStyle`. Satellite and Photos 1950 are not recoloured (as today's orthophoto). Carte d'état-major and Carte de 1950 are dimmed in dark with a static `raster-brightness-max` per scheme, **0.8 in dark, 1.0 in light**, as numbers in `theme-visual.ts`. The Types de forêt overlay is not dimmed. The incoming layer of a wipe takes the same treatment as when it is shown at rest. The bubble and the card use `mapPanel` with the theme's text, so in dark they are dense graphite with light text. **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**

## Layout and component contract

### 1. Capsule and locate arrow (unchanged shapes)

- The top-right capsule (`MapTopControls`, Liquid Glass on iOS 26, `mapControl.glass`) keeps its two buttons: the **globe** (basemap) button on top, the **download** button below. Same size, place and glyph logic as today (the globe shows `earth-outline` while Plan is displayed, `map-outline` otherwise).
- **The globe no longer switches Plan/Satellite on tap: it enters (or leaves) the basemap swipe mode** (section 3). `accessibilityLabel` "Changer de fond de carte", hint "Active le mode de changement de fond : glissez sur la carte", `accessibilityState={{ expanded: swipeModeOn }}`. Dot (8 pt moss) at its top-right while the base differs from the saved preference or a comparison is on; the a11y value then reads "fond modifié". It stays enabled during a comparison (a tap re-enters swipe mode).
- The download button opens the compact bottom card (section 6). It is rendered only when offline maps are enabled, as today.
- The locate arrow stays at the bottom right, unchanged; it lifts above the compact card while the card is open.
- The score legend, count pill and offline indicator at the bottom left are unchanged.

### 2. Overlay button and bubble

- **Button** (`testID="map-overlay-button"`): round 50 pt glass button under the capsule, glyph `layers-outline` at `mapControlIconSize`, `accessibilityLabel` "Superpositions : types de forêt et parcelles", hint "Ouvre les réglages des superpositions", `accessibilityState={{ expanded: bubbleOpen }}`. Dot while **Types de forêt is on or Parcelles is not Toutes**; a11y value "réglages modifiés". Always rendered (it does not depend on offline maps).
- **Bubble** (`testID="map-overlay-bubble"`, `GlassSurface surface={visual.mapPanel}`): opens beside the capsule, right edge 72 pt from the screen edge, top level with the button, 160 ms fade. Content, top to bottom, gap 12:
  1. Header "Superpositions" (13 SemiBold, secondary).
  2. **Types de forêt row** (min height 56): 40 pt tile `leaf-outline`, title "Types de forêt" (16 SemiBold), meta "Feuillus, conifères, mélangées" (13 Regular), native `Switch` on the right. When on, a **compact legend** under the row: the official legend image of the layer (300 x 69 aspect, bundled as an asset because it must show offline) at the bubble's content width on a paper tile, `accessibilityLabel` "Légende des types de forêt : feuillus, conifères, forêts mélangées, peupleraies, landes et forêts ouvertes". **[discretion, owner reviews on device; the planner confirms the class list against the asset and drops the image if it does not read]**. Offline and layer not downloaded for the view: row disabled, meta "Indisponible hors connexion", switch off and disabled.
  3. Header "Parcelles".
  4. **Three-state control** (`AccessibilityRole radiogroup`, `testID="overlay-parcels-all|ibp|none"`): three equal `AppChoiceChip` pills "Toutes" / "Avec IBP" / "Aucune", exactly one active, default Toutes. It covers the app's parcel polygons **and** the cadastre lines at once; the rendering of each state is the table in the Color section (**Toutes = cadastre lines plus all polygons, today's rendering; Avec IBP = only parcels with an IBP survey and no cadastre lines; Aucune = neither**).
  5. Helper line (13 Regular) of the chosen state: Toutes "Lignes du cadastre et toutes les parcelles."; Avec IBP "Seulement les parcelles qui ont un relevé IBP. Pas de lignes du cadastre."; Aucune "Ni lignes du cadastre ni parcelles. Les parcelles ne sont plus touchables."
- Changes apply to the map at once, no confirmation; haptic `feedback.selection()` on the switch and on each segment. Parcelles works offline (polygons come from local data; cadastre lines follow the offline packs).
- **Dismissal:** tapping the overlay button again, tapping the map (the tap only closes the bubble), or entering the swipe mode closes it. Opening the bubble closes the download card and **ends the swipe mode** (map unfrozen, comparison kept). The bubble does not freeze the map. While comparing, the overlays apply to **both sides** (a sentence under the Types de forêt row: "S'applique aux deux côtés de la comparaison.", only while comparing).
- There is no "Revenir à l'Explorer" control: the user undoes a state in place (switch off, Toutes, swipe back to the saved base); a cold start resets everything session-only.

### 3. Basemap swipe mode

**Entering:** tap the globe. The map **freezes**: pan, zoom, rotate, pitch and taps on markers or parcels are off on every map; a touch on the map only drives the wipe or exits. Entering closes the overlay bubble and the download card. Haptic none. The screen reader receives "Changement de fond. {nom}, {époque}."

**Top stack** (a column anchored at the capsule top and **centred on the screen horizontally**; items centred; gap 8; one labelled control only, "Terminé"):
1. **Indicator pill** (`testID="swipe-indicator"`, glass `mapControl.glass`, 36 high, max width 232): `chevron-back-outline` 14, the basemap name without its parenthesis (13 SemiBold: "Plan", "Satellite", "Carte d'état-major", "Carte de 1950", "Photos aériennes"), the era (12 Regular, secondary: "aujourd'hui", "aujourd'hui", "1820-1866", "vers 1950", "1950-1965"), `chevron-forward-outline` 14. Chevrons are cues, not buttons.
2. **Five dots** under the pill: one per basemap in cycle order; the current one larger (8 pt) and full white, the others 6 pt at 70%; an unavailable one (offline) at 40%. Decorative.
3. **"Terminé" pill** (`testID="swipe-done"`, glass, 40 high with hit slop to 44, word 12 SemiBold): the **single** control of the swipe mode, in a comparison as well. It **frees the map and keeps what is shown, including a comparison**; nothing else changes.
4. **Transient hint** (`visual.mapHint` scrim, 12 SemiBold white, 24 high): "Glissez · lâchez au milieu pour comparer", visible **3 s** then fading out (about 1 s; at once under Reduce Motion) on each entry. While a finger is down it shows the live message below instead.
5. The notices of section 8 follow below this stack, left to right centred.

**Wipe gesture** (reference: `swipe` engine of the kit; geometry in `wipe-geometry.ts`, rules in `swipe-machine.ts`):
- **Start:** a touch on the map starts a gesture. It becomes a wipe after **6 pt** of horizontal movement; before that it is a tap candidate. Vertical drift is ignored (the map is frozen).
- **Direction and order:** dragging **left** (finger moves toward the left edge) uncovers the **next** basemap of the cycle from the right, behind the finger; dragging **right** uncovers the **previous** one from the left. The cycle wraps (Photos 1950 then Plan). **Unavailable layers are skipped** (section 3b). The incoming layer is clipped to the strip between the finger and the edge it comes from; the current layer stays under it. No extra line is drawn at the wipe front.
- **Pill while dragging:** the indicator shows the **incoming** layer's name and era, with the live hint "Lâchez pour changer de fond" or "Lâchez pour comparer" (below).
- **Release thresholds**, measured as the revealed fraction `rev` of the screen width (0 to 1), when no comparison exists yet:
  | Release | Result |
  |---------|--------|
  | `rev < 0.2` | **Cancel**: the incoming layer springs back out in 240 ms (`brandMotion.durations.base`, standard easing); nothing changes |
  | `0.2 <= rev <= 0.8` | **Comparison**: both layers stay side by side, the wipe front becomes the curtain line at the release x (section 4). Hint while dragging in this band: "Lâchez pour comparer" |
  | `rev > 0.8` | **Commit**: the incoming layer finishes covering the screen in 240 ms and becomes the base. Hint: "Lâchez pour changer de fond" |
  Below 20% the hint line is hidden (release cancels, so "changer de fond" would be false): only the incoming name shows. **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**
- **Tap** (movement under 6 pt in both axes): exits the swipe mode (comparison, if any, stays).
- **Saved preference:** a commit to **Plan or Satellite** writes `map_basemap`; a commit to an old base is session-only; cancel and exit without commit change nothing. Entering the comparison writes nothing.
- **Haptics** (`feedback.selection()`): on commit, on the start of a comparison, on a side change, on leaving a comparison by the edge or by "Quitter la comparaison"; **[discretion]** one tick each time a drag crosses 20% or 80% (the hint flips). None for cancel, none while dragging otherwise.
- **Reduce Motion:** no wipe. The map stays still while the finger moves; the pill shows the incoming name and the hint; on release the result is applied **instantly** (cancel does nothing, comparison puts the line at the release x, commit swaps the base) with no completion or spring-back animation, and the hint appears and disappears without fade.

**Exits** (all return the map to free pan and zoom; none changes the base):
1. "Terminé".
2. A tap on the map (only when no comparison exists; with a comparison a tap does nothing).
3. A tap on the globe.
4. **8 s of inactivity** (timer restarts after every gesture end and every action; it does not run during a gesture). **It is disabled while a screen reader is on** (`AccessibilityInfo.isScreenReaderEnabled`).
5. Opening the overlay bubble or the download card, or leaving the Explorer tab.

**3b. Offline behaviour:** a layer is available offline only if the area at the view centre has its pack (Plan and Satellite are always listed; extra layers need a pack and a policy that downloaded them). In the swipe mode:
- Unavailable layers are **skipped** by the cycle and by the neighbour search, and their dot is dimmed.
- On entering swipe mode, if at least one layer is skipped, a short notice joins the stack for 3 s: "1 fond non téléchargé pour cette zone est ignoré." / "N fonds non téléchargés pour cette zone sont ignorés." (`accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"`).
- If no other layer is available, the wipe does not start and the hint reads "Aucun autre fond n'est disponible hors connexion ici."
- The current base never switches by itself when the connection drops: the notices of section 8 say so.

**Accessibility alternative (the swipe has a non-gesture path):**
- The indicator pill is `accessibilityRole="adjustable"` with value "{nom}, {époque}, fond {n} sur 5" and the custom actions **"Fond suivant"** (`increment`), **"Fond précédent"** (`decrement`) and **"Comparer avec le fond suivant"** (starts a comparison with the next available layer on the right, line at 50%). Each activation changes the base with the same rules as a commit (saved preference included) and announces the new name and era. The globe, "Terminé" and "Quitter la comparaison" are plain labelled buttons. With a screen reader on, the idle exit is off, so the user is never ejected mid-action.

### 4. Comparison by release in the middle

There is **no Comparer button**. The comparison is created by releasing a full-width swipe between 20% and 80% (section 3). Two states combine with the swipe mode:

| State | Map | Top display |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| Swipe mode, no comparison | Frozen, one layer | Indicator pill, dots, "Terminé", hint (section 3) |
| Comparison, swipe mode on | Frozen, curtain visible | **No indicator pill**; the single centred "Terminé" pill; the 3 s hint "Glissez un côté pour changer son fond · tirez la ligne au bord pour quitter la comparaison"; side tags with chevrons. No quit pill (quitting is by dragging the line to an edge, or "Terminé" then "Quitter la comparaison") |
| Comparison, swipe mode off | Free (pan and zoom on the left map, the right map follows); curtain visible | **One centred pill "Quitter la comparaison"** (`close-outline` 16 + label), visible **only while the map is free**; plain side tags |
| Free, no comparison | Today's Explorer | Nothing |

- **Created layers:** dragging left and releasing in the band gives A (left) = the layer that was shown, B (right) = the incoming layer; dragging right gives A = the incoming layer, B = the layer that was shown. The line stays at the release x (clamped to 22 pt from each edge). No entrance animation (the line is already under the finger). Markers hide; a transient pill joins the notices column: "Les relevés sont masqués pendant la comparaison." for 4 s. Overlays (Types de forêt, Parcelles state) apply to both sides. The screen reader receives "Comparaison : {gauche} à gauche, {droite} à droite. Relevés masqués."
- **Side wipes (swipe mode on):** a swipe that starts on a side changes **that side's layer only**. The incoming layer wipes in **within that side** (clipped to the side's bounds, never crossing the line). Dragging left uncovers the next layer, right the previous one, in the same cycle, **skipping the layer shown on the other side** (never the same layer on both sides) and the unavailable ones. Release: revealed fraction of the side `> 1/3` commits (240 ms), otherwise it springs back (240 ms). No comparison creation from a side swipe. Hint while dragging: "Lâchez pour changer de fond". A side change never writes the saved preference. Reduce Motion: instant, as in section 3.
- **The line is dragged by hand**: the 44 pt strip with the round knob (section 5), clamped to `[22, width - 22]` while dragging, at any time (swipe mode on or off). No snap, no haptic while dragging.
- **Quitting by the edge:** releasing the line **within 40 pt of an edge** ends the comparison **keeping the visible layer** (line near the left edge: the right layer B remains; near the right edge: the left layer A remains). Immediate (the finger is already there). Haptic selection.
- **Quitting by the pill:** the centred pill "Quitter la comparaison" (`testID="compare-quit"`, top, level with the capsule top, hidden while the swipe mode is on) ends the comparison and returns to a single basemap, keeping **side A** (the left layer, the base that was underneath); the line slides to the right edge in 240 ms (instant under Reduce Motion). **[discretion, owner reviews on device: "the visible layer" is read as side A, as in the sketch engine]**
- There is **no round or unlabelled exit control** anywhere, and no top-left control.
- If the layer kept ends up being Plan or Satellite, it becomes the **saved preference** (it is a chosen base, like a commit). **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**
- **"Terminé" in a comparison** frees the map (swipe mode off) and **keeps the comparison**; the "Quitter la comparaison" pill then appears in its place. The globe re-enters swipe mode (the comparison stays, frozen) and the pill gives way to "Terminé".
- **Tapping the map** in a comparison with swipe mode on does nothing (it would otherwise end the mode while the user aims at a side); the exit paths are "Terminé", the globe and 8 s of inactivity (swipe mode only; the comparison stays).
- **Markers and clusters are hidden** while comparing (a `ViewAnnotation` belongs to one map). Parcel polygons per the Parcelles state and the cadastre lines (Toutes only) are drawn on both maps. The count pill stays; the legend stays (parcels still use score colours).
- **Interplay:** the overlay bubble opens normally during a comparison (it ends the swipe mode, keeps the comparison). The download card likewise. The download **chip** stays visible during a comparison; it is hidden while the swipe mode is on (see section 6).
- **Accessibility alternative for the sides:** the handle (`adjustable`) carries the custom actions "Fond suivant à gauche", "Fond précédent à gauche", "Fond suivant à droite", "Fond précédent à droite" and "Quitter la comparaison", plus `increment`/`decrement` to move the line by 10 points.
- **If the two-map spike (curtain and wipe share it) is a no-go**, the wipe degrades to the instant change (as under Reduce Motion) and the comparison is not built until the owner decides; the rest of the contract stands.

### 8. Notices column (over-zoom, coverage, offline, error)

**Over-zoom (locked: scaled tiles, never a blank map).** Every raster source declares `maxzoom`, so MapLibre scales the last tile. Rule: a pill appears when any layer shown is scaled by 4 or more, i.e. when `mapZoom >= maxTileZoom + 1` (256 pt tiles are read one level above the map zoom): from map zoom 16 for Carte d'état-major and Carte de 1950 (tile zoom 15), 17 for Types de forêt (16), and for Photos 1950 from 19 online and 18 offline (offline cap 17, locked). Text "Fond peu détaillé à cette échelle" (a base) or "Superposition peu détaillée à cette échelle" (the overlay); the base text wins when both apply. Glyph `information-circle-outline` 16, non-interactive, `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"`, 160 ms fade, disappears when the zoom goes back under the threshold. No zoom lock, no automatic jump to another layer. (A frozen map cannot zoom, so the pill appears when the swipe mode is left or in a free comparison.)

**Outside coverage.** A layer outside its coverage answers 404 (transparent), so the base ground shows. When the view centre leaves a shown layer's coverage box (catalogue): "{name} ne couvre pas cette zone." with `alert-circle-outline`. Photos 1950-1965 and Carte de 1950 cover France and most overseas departments; Carte d'état-major and Types de forêt cover mainland France.

**Offline, layer shown but not downloaded here.** With no connectivity and a shown layer without a pack at the view centre: persistent pill "{name} n'est pas téléchargée pour cette zone." with `cloud-offline-outline`; the base stays (no silent switch). Several: one pill per layer, at most two, then "Plusieurs couches ne sont pas téléchargées pour cette zone."

**Online load error.** "Impossible d'afficher {name}." with a trailing "Réessayer" link (13 SemiBold, 44 pt target; reloads the style).

**Position.** The notices form one column, top-centre (centred on the screen like the mode controls, never reaching the capsule area 72 from the right), starting at **the capsule's top**. They stack **below the top mode control** when there is one: the swipe stack when the swipe mode is on (indicator, dots, "Terminé", hint, then notices), the "Quitter la comparaison" pill in a free comparison, and **below the download chip's row** (capsule top + 51 + 5 + 40 + 8) while a chip is shown. Order: transient hidden-markers pill, skipped-layers pill, coverage / offline / error pills, over-zoom pill. They never overlap the capsule, the overlay button or the bubble (right of 72), nor the top mode controls.

## Accessibility Contract

- Targets: every pressable at least 44 x 44 (capsule and overlay buttons 50, Parcelles segments 44, "Terminé" and "Quitter la comparaison" 40 with hit slop to 44, card close 44, card button 46, chip 40 with hit slop, line strip 44, knob 44). The indicator pill, dots, hint, tags and the running chip are not interactive.
- Roles and labels: globe `button` with `expanded` and an active value; overlay button `button` with `expanded` and an active value; Types de forêt row with a native `Switch`; Parcelles `radiogroup` of `radio` chips; indicator `adjustable` with actions "Fond suivant", "Fond précédent", "Comparer avec le fond suivant"; "Terminé" `button`; "Quitter la comparaison" `button` (visible label, a11y hint "Revient à un seul fond, celui de gauche"); no unlabelled control anywhere; line strip `adjustable` with the left / right actions; chip `progressbar` while running, `button` when failed or partial; decorative glyphs, dots and chevrons hidden from accessibility.
- **The swipe has a non-gesture alternative**: the indicator's actions and the line's actions cover every outcome of the wipe (change base, start a comparison, change a side, quit). The idle exit is disabled when a screen reader is on.
- Announcements: entering swipe mode, every base change, comparison start (live, once), the skipped-layers notice, over-zoom, coverage, offline and error pills (`accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` on Android, `announceForAccessibility` on iOS, once when they appear); the download start, each 25% step and the outcome come from the screen (there is no open sheet to speak).
- Focus order on the map: the centred top control ("Terminé" with the indicator while swiping, or "Quitter la comparaison" in a free comparison), capsule (globe, download), overlay button, bubble content when open, locate arrow, line strip, then the rest.
- Dynamic Type: bubble and card wrap; on-map pills and tags truncate at the tail. No clipped text at AX3 in the bubble or the card.
- Contrast: bubble and card text on `mapPanel` (verified by the glass tests in both schemes); pills and tags on `mapControl.glass` (`visual-tokens.test.ts`); the hint's white ink on its scrim at 4.5:1 over the lightest basemap (added test); the curtain line and the swipe dots over the three reference backdrops at 3:1 non-text (added test); the two dots against the capsule and overlay glass at 3:1 (added test); the lightened parcel outline over each old base at 3:1 (added test; tune the 0.6 factor against it). Colour is never the only carrier: unavailable layers have a dimmed dot and a sentence; the state of Parcelles has a helper sentence.
- Reduced motion: no wipe animation (instant result), no spring-back, curtain exit instant, tags, pills, hint and chip appear without fade; line dragging is direct manipulation and stays.
