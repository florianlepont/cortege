## Scope of the contract

One screen (Explorer, `PublicMapScreen`) plus one row in Paramètres. **The Explorer's design is kept, with one rearrangement of the top-right buttons (owner item 13 a): the capsule holds the globe (basemap) button and the overlay (layers) button together; the download button is a separate round button on its own below the capsule.** The locate arrow stays at the bottom right. Everything else appears on demand.

| Surface | What this contract defines |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Capsule (globe + overlay), download button, locate arrow | Positions, dots, states (section 1) |
| Overlay bubble | Types de forêt switch with legend, Parcelles Toutes / Avec IBP / Aucune (section 2) |
| Basemap swipe mode | Indicator pill, "Terminé", transient hint, wipe gesture, freezing, exits (section 3, 3b offline) |
| Immersive effect (swipe mode only) | Receding map window, shadow, buttons fading out, motion, interplay (section 3c) |
| Comparison by release in the middle | Centre line with a slim grabber (exit gesture), tappable side tags, free map, "Quitter la comparaison", markers hidden (section 4) |
| Curtain look | Line, side tags (section 5) |
| Compact bottom card and chip | Estimate, "Télécharger", chip-only progress, failed and partial results (section 6) |
| Paramètres > Cartes | "Couches hors ligne" row and its action sheet (section 7) |
| Top notices column, legend and attribution, states | Over-zoom, coverage, offline, error, sources (sections 8 to 10) |

**No "Couches" pill, no big panel, no Comparer button, no basemap bottom card, no thumbnails chooser, no sheet-based download view, no "N relevés ici" count pill, no round glass knob, no free dragging of the line (it drags only as an exit gesture), no "Terminé" inside the comparison.**

Base keys: `BasemapKey = "map" | "satellite"` stays for the picker, the PDF and the saved preference. The swipe layers use `MapLayerKey = "map" | "satellite" | "etat-major" | "carte-1950" | "photos-1950"` for bases and `"types-foret"` for the overlay. Order of the basemap cycle (locked): **Plan, Satellite, Carte d'état-major, Carte de 1950, Photos aériennes (1950-1965)**, wrapping around.

Study state is `{ base, overlay, parcels, compare: { sideA, sideB } | null, swipeMode }` with `parcels: "all" | "withIbp" | "none"`, held in `useMapLayersState`. The swipe mode and the comparison **never coexist**: the globe is disabled while comparing, and a comparison is created by the release that ends the swipe mode. **Persistence:** a **Plan or Satellite** chosen by the swipe writes the saved basemap preference (`map_basemap`). The **old bases**, the overlay, Parcelles and the comparison are **session-only**: reset on cold start, kept while the app lives and across tab changes (tabs stay mounted). "Default" means: base = the saved preference, overlay off, Parcelles = Toutes, no comparison. **Bare basemaps (owner, 2026-10-12):** while the swipe mode is on, no map draws the forest types, the cadastre lines or the parcels (the same layer components stay mounted, disabled or empty); the comparison keeps them on both sides. A Plan pack file whose style already carries the cadastre cannot be made bare.

## Color

All values come from the theme; light and dark follow the system only. Basemaps are not scheme aware (they stay light in dark mode) except the recoloured Plan, so every control over a map takes a dense surface.

| Role | Token | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | the map itself (raster basemap) | Not themed. Behind it `brandMapTokens.darkBasemapBackground` (`#0E1512`) as ground in dark |
| Secondary (30%) | `visual.mapControl.glass` (capsule, download button, indicator pill, "Terminé", "Quitter la comparaison", side tags, notice pills, chip); `visual.mapPanel` with the theme's text (overlay bubble, compact card) | Every control and card over the map |
| Accent (10%) | `visual.tab.dot` (moss) for the two dots; `visual.accentText` for links; native `Switch` on-track | Reserved list below |
| Destructive | not used | The failed download glyph uses `onSurface.danger`, glyph only |

Accent reserved for (exhaustive):
1. The 8 pt dot on the globe button (base differs from the saved one, or a comparison is on).
2. The 8 pt dot on the overlay button (Types de forêt on, or Parcelles other than Toutes).
3. The "Réessayer" links (notices and the failed card).
4. The leading tile glyph of the bubble row (`visual.glass.iconTint`).
5. The `Switch` on state.

(The active Parcelles segment uses the `AppChoiceChip` active style; chips are not accent, charter 13.4.)

Other colour rules:
- **Comparison line and grabber**: white core `visual.curtain.line`, edges `visual.curtain.edge` (graphite at about 45% alpha), same in both schemes, declared per scheme in `theme-visual.ts`; the grabber uses the same two tokens (no glass fill, no glyph). The swipe dots reuse them (white fill, 1 pt edge ring).
- **Hint scrim**: `visual.mapHint { fill, ink }`, graphite at 70% alpha or more with a white ink (the engine uses 55%; the planner raises it until white text holds 4.5:1 over the lightest basemap, tested). **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**
- **Immersive window tokens** (`visual.immersive { shadow: { color, opacity, radius, offsetY }, ring }`, per scheme in `theme-visual.ts`): light = a soft shadow (about opacity 0.18, radius 24, offsetY 8) with no ring; dark = no usable shadow on the dark canvas, so a 1 pt hairline ring (`mapControl.hairline` level) plus a faint shadow. The window sits on `colors.canvas`. The basemaps stay light in dark mode, so the receded window reads as a light card on a dark canvas. **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**
- Offline and error lines: `cloud-offline-outline` / `alert-circle-outline` (error in `onSurface.danger`), text in secondary colour; no red text, no tinted box.
- **Parcels and cadastre on the map (Parcelles control).** Same on the left and right map while comparing:

  | State | Cadastre lines (raster) | Parcel polygons (fill + outline) | Taps |
  |-------|-------------------------|----------------------------------|------|
  | Toutes (default) | Drawn from zoom 15, opacity 0.9 (0.7 on Carte d'état-major and Carte de 1950) | All the polygons the Explorer draws today, with today's tokens: score-band fill for a scored parcel, warm grey (`parcelUnscored`) for one without a usable score, neutral for the others | As today |

  Toutes is today's rendering. A parcel focus coming from the search (Phase 25, selected terracotta) is **always drawn**, whatever the state, so a result never lands on an empty map.
- **Z-order** (bottom to top): base, Types de forêt (opacity 0.55), cadastre lines (Toutes only), parcel casing, parcel fill, parcel outline, markers (hidden while comparing). Green still means a high score only.
- **Parcels on the old bases.** The three old bases (Carte d'état-major, Carte de 1950, Photos 1950) look washed out under today's fill and a white casing. On them, in both schemes (they are light in dark mode too), the parcel **fill alpha is multiplied by `brandMapTokens.parcelFillOldBaseFactor` = 0.6** (a score-band fill drops from 0.55 to about 0.33; outlines keep width and colour). `brandMapTokens.parcelCasing` (white at about 85% alpha) is drawn under `parcels-outline` with width `strokeWidth + 2`, **only** on the old bases and when Types de forêt is on, with the same 0.6 factor on the old bases. Plan and Satellite without the overlay keep today's exact rendering, in both schemes. **[discretion, owner reviews on device: one number to tune]**
- **Dark mode of the rasters:** Plan keeps `darkenPlanIgnStyle`. Satellite and Photos 1950 are not recoloured (as today's orthophoto). Carte d'état-major and Carte de 1950 are dimmed in dark with a static `raster-brightness-max` per scheme, **0.8 in dark, 1.0 in light**, as numbers in `theme-visual.ts`. The Types de forêt overlay is not dimmed. The incoming layer of a wipe takes the same treatment as when it is shown at rest. The bubble and the card use `mapPanel` with the theme's text, so in dark they are dense graphite with light text. **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**

### 3. Basemap swipe mode

**Entering:** tap the globe (not available while comparing). The map **freezes**: pan, zoom, rotate, pitch and taps on markers or parcels are off; a touch on the map only drives the wipe or exits. Entering closes the overlay bubble and the download card, and starts the **immersive effect** (section 3c). Haptic none. The screen reader receives "Changement de fond. {nom}, {époque}."

**Top stack** (a column anchored at `capsuleTop` and **centred on the screen horizontally**; items centred; gap 8; one labelled control only, "Terminé"):
1. **Indicator pill** (`testID="swipe-indicator"`, glass `mapControl.glass`, 36 high, max width 232): `chevron-back-outline` 14, the basemap name without its parenthesis (13 SemiBold: "Plan", "Satellite", "Carte d'état-major", "Carte de 1950", "Photos aériennes"), the era (12 Regular, secondary: "aujourd'hui", "aujourd'hui", "1820-1866", "vers 1950", "1950-1965"), `chevron-forward-outline` 14. Chevrons are cues, not buttons.
2. **Five dots** under the pill: one per basemap in cycle order; the current one larger (8 pt) and full white, the others 6 pt at 70%; an unavailable one (offline) at 40%. Decorative.
3. **"Terminé" pill** (`testID="swipe-done"`, glass, 40 high with hit slop to 44, word 12 SemiBold): the **single** control of the swipe mode. It **frees the map and keeps what is shown**; nothing else changes.
4. **Transient hint** (`visual.mapHint` scrim, 12 SemiBold white, min 24 high): "Glissez · lâchez au milieu pour comparer", visible **3 s** then fading out (about 1 s; at once under Reduce Motion) on each entry. While a finger is down it shows the live message of the zone instead.
5. The notices of section 8 follow below this stack, left to right centred.

**Wipe gesture** (reference: `swipe` engine of the kit; geometry in `wipe-geometry.ts`, rules in `swipe-machine.ts`). The gesture is measured on the **receded window** (section 3c): the revealed fraction is relative to the window's width, and a touch in the margin around the window counts as a touch at its nearest edge.
- **Start:** a touch on the map starts a gesture. It becomes a wipe after **6 pt** of horizontal movement; before that it is a tap candidate. Vertical drift is ignored (the map is frozen).
- **Direction and order:** dragging **left** uncovers the **next** basemap of the cycle from the right, behind the finger; dragging **right** uncovers the **previous** one from the left. The cycle wraps (Photos 1950 then Plan). **Unavailable layers are skipped** (section 3b). The incoming layer is clipped to the strip between the finger and the edge it comes from; the current layer stays under it. No extra line is drawn at the wipe front.
- **Pill while dragging:** the indicator shows the **incoming** layer's name and era, with the live hint of the zone the finger is in ("Lâchez pour revenir", "Lâchez pour comparer" or "Lâchez pour changer de fond").
- **Zones (owner decision 11): the revealed fraction `rev` (0 to 1) falls in three equal zones**, and the release outcome is the zone the finger is in:
  | Zone | `rev` | Release result | Live hint while the finger is in it |
  |------|-------|----------------|-------------------------------------|
  | **Cancel** | below 1/3 | The incoming layer springs back out in 240 ms (`brandMotion.durations.base`, standard easing); nothing changes | "Lâchez pour revenir" |
  | **Compare** | 1/3 to 2/3 | **Comparison**: both layers stay side by side; the wipe front becomes the comparison line, which eases to the exact centre (section 4) | "Lâchez pour comparer" |
  | **Commit** | above 2/3 | The incoming layer finishes covering the screen in 240 ms and becomes the base | "Lâchez pour changer de fond" |
  - **Hysteresis 0.03:** a boundary is crossed upward at exactly 1/3 or 2/3 and crossed back downward only below the boundary minus 0.03, so the hint, the haptic and the outcome never flicker when the finger rests on a boundary. The zone current at release decides.
  - The hint line is never hidden: it changes text with the zone. The indicator pill keeps the incoming layer's name and era in every zone.
- **Tap** (movement under 6 pt in both axes): exits the swipe mode.
- **Saved preference:** a commit to **Plan or Satellite** writes `map_basemap`; a commit to an old base is session-only; cancel and exit without commit change nothing. Entering the comparison writes nothing.

**Haptics of the swipe (all through `ui/feedback.ts`; none while the finger stays inside a zone):**
| Event | Haptic |
|-------|--------|
| The finger **enters the compare zone** (from the cancel zone or from the commit zone) | `feedback.impact.medium()`, the clear **detent** |
| The finger enters the **commit** zone, or returns to the **cancel** zone | `feedback.selection()` (light tick) |
| Release that **opens the comparison** | `feedback.impact.medium()` |
| Release that **commits** a new basemap | `feedback.selection()` |
| Release that **cancels** | nothing |
| Gesture start (first 6 pt, zone cancel) | nothing |
| Entering or leaving the swipe mode, the immersive effect | nothing |
| The accessibility actions "Fond suivant" / "Fond précédent" (as a commit) and "Comparer avec le fond suivant" | `feedback.selection()` (the compare action: `feedback.impact.medium()`) |
Haptics fire at the moment the zone changes (after hysteresis), never repeatedly. Reduce Motion and screen-reader behaviour are unchanged: Reduce Motion removes the wipe animation, not the haptics (the zone logic and the hint still run on the finger position), and the accessibility actions already cover the non-gesture path.

- **Reduce Motion:** no wipe. The map stays still while the finger moves; the pill shows the incoming name and the hint; on release the result is applied **instantly** (cancel does nothing, comparison puts the line at the centre, commit swaps the base) with no completion or spring-back animation, and the hint appears and disappears without fade.

**Exits** (all return the map to free pan and zoom and end the immersive effect; none changes the base):
1. "Terminé".
2. A tap on the map.
3. A tap on the globe.
4. **8 s of inactivity** (timer restarts after every gesture end and every action; it does not run during a gesture). **It is disabled while a screen reader is on** (`AccessibilityInfo.isScreenReaderEnabled`).
5. Opening the overlay bubble or the download card, or leaving the Explorer tab.

**3b. Offline behaviour (owner rule "règle simple complète", 2026-10-12, supersedes the centre rule):** online every layer is available; offline a layer (Plan and Satellite included, the overlay too) is available only if the whole visible viewport lies inside a ready area that owns its pack, evaluated on the settled view (300 ms debounce). The carousel pill shows it (cloud-done-outline downloaded, cloud-offline-outline not; read aloud). **The comparison needs a connection (owner, 2026-10-12):** offline the swipe has two zones (cancel, commit), the intro hint reads "La comparaison demande une connexion", no compare action is offered, the side tags cannot step, and a comparison that is on when the connection drops ends at once keeping the left layer (as "Quitter la comparaison"). The notices keep one offline pill. In the swipe mode:
- Unavailable layers are **skipped** by the cycle and by the neighbour search, and their dot is dimmed.
- On entering swipe mode, if at least one layer is skipped, a short notice would join the stack for 3 s, but **the notices are hidden in the swipe mode, so it is not drawn there**: "1 fond non téléchargé pour cette zone est ignoré." / "N fonds non téléchargés pour cette zone sont ignorés." (`accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"`).
- If no other layer is available, the wipe does not start and the hint reads "Aucun autre fond n'est disponible hors connexion ici."
- The current base never switches by itself when the connection drops: the notices of section 8 say so.

**Accessibility alternative (the swipe has a non-gesture path):**
- The indicator pill is `accessibilityRole="adjustable"` with value "{nom}, {époque}, fond {n} sur 5" and the custom actions **"Fond suivant"** (`increment`), **"Fond précédent"** (`decrement`) and **"Comparer avec le fond suivant"** (starts a comparison with the next available layer on the right, line at the centre). Each activation changes the base with the same rules as a commit (saved preference included) and announces the new name and era. The globe and "Terminé" are plain labelled buttons. With a screen reader on, the idle exit is off, so the user is never ejected mid-action.

### 3c. Immersive effect (swipe mode only)

The swipe mode turns the screen into a focused "choose a basemap" scene. **It applies to the swipe mode only, never to the comparison** (in the comparison the map is full size and the buttons are visible).

- **The map window recedes** like a card: the whole map view area (the Explorer's map container, above the tab bar) **scales to 0.94 about its centre**, its corners round to `brandRadius.card` (22), and a **soft shadow** appears under it (tokens `visual.immersive`, Color section), over the app background `colors.canvas`. The window stays centred, so the margins are about 3% of its width and height on each side. The tab bar is system chrome and is untouched.
- **Everything that is a map button fades out** (opacity to 0, `pointerEvents="none"`, hidden from accessibility): the **capsule** (globe and overlay), the **download button**, the **locate arrow**, the **legend (i) button and its expanded panel** and the offline badge of the bottom-left row. The overlay bubble and the download card are closed on entry. The **download chip is hidden too** (the download goes on; the chip returns on exit, a failed or partial chip included, and the percentage announcements continue).
- **What remains:** the **top stack** (indicator pill, dots, "Terminé", hint) in screen coordinates (not scaled), level with `capsuleTop`, and the **notices column** below it (the notices describe the layers being swiped: skipped layers, over-zoom, coverage, offline, error). **[discretion, owner reviews on device: the notices stay]** The incoming wipe layer is inside the window, so its corners are clipped by the rounded window.
- **Motion** (`brandMotion` tokens, `ReduceMotion.System` on every timing):
  | Moment | Window (scale, radius, shadow) | Buttons | Top stack |
  |--------|--------------------------------|---------|-----------|
  | Enter | 360 ms (`durations.slow`), `easings.decelerate` | Fade out in 160 ms (`durations.fast`) from t = 0 | Fade in 160 ms, delayed 80 ms |
  | Exit (any exit path) | 240 ms (`durations.base`), `easings.standard` | Fade in 160 ms, starting 80 ms after the exit begins | Fade out 160 ms from t = 0 |
  | Comparison starts (release in the compare zone) | Returns to full size in 240 ms **while the line eases to the centre in the same 240 ms** | As Exit | As Exit; the side tags and the "Quitter la comparaison" pill fade in 160 ms |
  | **Reduce Motion** | Instant: the final state is shown at once (no scale animation, no fades) | Instant | Instant |
- **Haptics:** unchanged by the effect (none on entering or leaving).
- **Dark mode:** the window sits on the dark canvas; the light basemap reads as a light card; the shadow is replaced by the hairline ring (Color section).
- **Platform notes:** the scale is a transform on a wrapper (UI thread); the rounded corners need the map clipped by its wrapper, which shares the **device spike** (on Android a `surface` map may ignore the clip: use `androidView="texture"` or accept square corners there). Below Android API 28 the outset shadow is lost (charter 13.9, accepted). The two-map wipe and the immersive window share the same spike.

### 4. Comparison by release in the middle (one single mode)

There is **no Comparer button**. The comparison is created by releasing a full-width swipe in the middle third (between 1/3 and 2/3 of the way, section 3). **There is a single comparison mode** (owner item 13 d): the owner wants to change the layers and to zoom and pan at the same time, so the map is **free** and each side is changed by its own tag.

| State | Map | Top display |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| Swipe mode (no comparison) | Frozen, one layer, immersive | Indicator pill, dots, "Terminé", hint (section 3) |
| Comparison | **Free**: pan and zoom on the left map (the right map follows); rotation and pitch stay locked; full size; buttons visible | One centred pill **"Quitter la comparaison"** (`close-outline` 16 + label); two tappable side tags; the line with its grabber, draggable only as an exit gesture |
| Free, no comparison | Today's Explorer | Nothing |

- **Created layers:** dragging left and releasing in the compare zone gives A (left) = the layer that was shown, B (right) = the incoming layer; dragging right gives A = the incoming layer, B = the layer that was shown. The line **follows the wipe front** during the swipe, then **eases to the exact centre** in 240 ms (standard easing; instantly under Reduce Motion) and rests there; at rest it can be moved by hand only as an exit gesture (below). The swipe mode and its immersive effect end at the release (section 3c). Markers and clusters are **hidden silently** while comparing (no notice). Overlays (Types de forêt, Parcelles state) apply to both sides. The screen reader receives "Comparaison : {gauche} à gauche, {droite} à droite."
- **Changing a side (tags):** each side tag (section 5) changes **that side's layer only**. A tap on the **back chevron** selects the **previous** layer of the cycle for that side, a tap on the **forward chevron** the **next** one; a **horizontal swipe on the tag** does the same (swipe left = next, swipe right = previous, as in the wipe; a travel of at least 44 pt or a flick commits). The change is shown as a wipe **confined to that side's half of the fixed centre** (240 ms: the new layer enters from the half's right bound for next, from its left bound for previous; instantly under Reduce Motion). The cycle **skips the layer shown on the other side** (never the same layer on both sides) and the unavailable ones; when no other layer is available the chevrons are dimmed and disabled. Haptic `feedback.selection()` on each change. A side change never writes the saved preference. The screen reader receives "{Côté gauche|Côté droit} : {nom}, {époque}." **[discretion, owner reviews on device: the swipe distance on the tag]**
- **Free map:** the comparison does not freeze anything. Panning or zooming works as in the Explorer (the left map is interactive, the right map follows its camera, rotation and pitch locked). The Plan, parcels and cadastre follow the Parcelles state on both sides.
- **Quitting:** the centred pill **"Quitter la comparaison"** (`testID="compare-quit"`, at the **bottom**, centred, just above the tab bar like the swipe hint; its edge-exit hint sits above it; a card or bubble that opens draws over it; owner, 2026-10-12) is the **one** top control. It returns to a single basemap keeping **side A** (the left layer, the base that was underneath): the right half's layer wipes out to the right edge together with the line in 240 ms (instant under Reduce Motion). If the layer kept is Plan or Satellite it becomes the **saved preference**. Haptic `feedback.selection()`. **[discretion, owner reviews on device: "keeping side A" is the sketch engine's reading of "the visible layer"]** (The grabber drag is the other exit and keeps the layer on the side opposite to the edge.)
- **The line as an exit gesture only (owner item 13 b, corrected).** Dragging the **grabber** (the slim bar on the line) moves the line with the finger, clamped to `[22, width - 22]`; the side tags follow the line (24 pt from it) and fade out while a side is under 72 pt wide. The map and the tags are not active during the drag.
  - **Edge exit zones:** the **outer fifth of the width on each side** (`x < width / 5` or `x > 4 width / 5`). **Entering a zone fires `feedback.selection()` (a tick)**; leaving it does not; re-entering ticks again. While the finger is in a zone, the hint "Lâchez pour quitter la comparaison" shows under the "Quitter la comparaison" pill (hint scrim, 12 SemiBold white). **[discretion, owner reviews on device]**
  - **Release outside a zone:** the line **springs back to the exact centre** in 240 ms (standard easing; instant under Reduce Motion); nothing else changes, no haptic.
  - **Release inside a zone:** the comparison ends and the **layer that covers the visible side is kept**: line dragged to the **left** edge keeps the **right** layer (B), line dragged to the **right** edge keeps the **left** layer (A). Immediate (the finger is already there); `feedback.selection()`; Plan or Satellite kept becomes the saved preference.
  - Only the 44 x 44 pt grabber hit area takes the touch, so panning and zooming the rest of the map are unaffected.
- **There is no "Terminé" in the comparison and no round or unlabelled exit control.** The exits are the grabber drag into an edge zone and the pill.
- **The globe is disabled** (dimmed) while comparing, so the swipe mode and the comparison never overlap.
- **Interplay:** the overlay bubble opens normally during a comparison (overlays apply to both sides). The download card likewise; the download button stays enabled, and the download **chip stays visible** (the comparison is not immersive). The bubble hides the chip while open (section 2). The legend (i) button stays visible and works; parcels still use score colours.
- **If the two-map spike (comparison, wipe and immersive window share it) is a no-go**, the wipe degrades to the instant change (as under Reduce Motion) and the comparison is not built until the owner decides; the rest of the contract stands.

### 8. Notices column (over-zoom, coverage, offline, error)

**Over-zoom (locked: scaled tiles, never a blank map).** Every raster source declares `maxzoom`, so MapLibre scales the last tile. Rule: a pill appears when any layer shown is scaled by 4 or more, i.e. when `mapZoom >= maxTileZoom + 1` (256 pt tiles are read one level above the map zoom): from map zoom 16 for Carte d'état-major and Carte de 1950 (tile zoom 15), 17 for Types de forêt (16), and for Photos 1950 from 19 online and 18 offline (offline cap 17, locked). Text "Fond peu détaillé à cette échelle" (a base) or "Superposition peu détaillée à cette échelle" (the overlay); the base text wins when both apply. Glyph `information-circle-outline` 16, non-interactive, `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"`, 160 ms fade, disappears when the zoom goes back under the threshold. No zoom lock, no automatic jump to another layer. (A frozen map cannot zoom, so the pill appears when the swipe mode is left, or in a comparison.)

**Outside coverage.** A layer outside its coverage answers 404 (transparent), so the base ground shows. When the view centre leaves a shown layer's coverage box (catalogue): "{name} ne couvre pas cette zone." with `alert-circle-outline`. Photos 1950-1965 and Carte de 1950 cover France and most overseas departments; Carte d'état-major and Types de forêt cover mainland France.

**Offline, layer shown but not downloaded here.** With no connectivity and a shown layer without a pack at the view centre: persistent pill "{name} n'est pas téléchargée pour cette zone." with `cloud-offline-outline`; the base stays (no silent switch). Several: one pill per layer, at most two, then "Plusieurs couches ne sont pas téléchargées pour cette zone."

**Online load error.** "Impossible d'afficher {name}." with a trailing "Réessayer" link (13 SemiBold, 44 pt target; reloads the style).

**Position.** The notices form one column, top-centre (centred on the screen like the mode controls, between x 71 and x 303 on a 375 pt screen, never reaching the right column of buttons at x 311), starting at **`capsuleTop`**. They stack **below the top mode control** when there is one: the swipe stack when the swipe mode is on (indicator, dots, "Terminé", hint); **the notices are hidden while the swipe mode is on (owner, 2026-10-12) and come back when it ends**, the "Quitter la comparaison" pill in a comparison, and **below the download chip's row** (`capsuleTop + 154 + 8 = capsuleTop + 162`) while a chip is shown (the chip occupies x 72 to ~300 from the right, in the same horizontal range). Order: skipped-layers pill, coverage / offline / error pills, over-zoom pill. They never overlap the capsule, the download button, the bubble (right of 72), nor the top mode controls. There is no hidden-markers notice (owner item 13 e).

## Accessibility Contract

- Targets: every pressable at least 44 x 44 (capsule and download buttons 50, Parcelles segments 44, side tags 44 high with 44 x 44 chevron targets, the grabber's 44 x 44 hit area, "Terminé" and "Quitter la comparaison" 40 with hit slop to 44, card close 44, card button 46, chip 40 with hit slop). The indicator pill, dots, hint, the line and the running chip are not interactive.
- Roles and labels: globe `button` with `expanded`, `disabled` while comparing and an active value; overlay button `button` with `expanded` and an active value; Types de forêt row with a native `Switch`; Parcelles `radiogroup` of `radio` chips; indicator `adjustable` with actions "Fond suivant", "Fond précédent", "Comparer avec le fond suivant"; "Terminé" `button`; each **side tag `adjustable`** ("Côté gauche : {nom}") with the actions "Fond suivant" and "Fond précédent", its chevrons hidden from accessibility; "Quitter la comparaison" `button` (visible label, hint "Revient à un seul fond, celui de gauche"); no unlabelled control anywhere; the comparison **grabber is `adjustable`** ("Séparation de la comparaison") with the two exit actions, the line itself is decorative and hidden; chip `progressbar` while running, `button` when failed or partial; decorative glyphs, dots and chevrons hidden from accessibility.
- **The swipe has a non-gesture alternative**: the indicator's actions cover every outcome of the wipe (change base, start a comparison), and the side tags' actions change either side; "Quitter la comparaison" and the grabber's two exit actions leave. The idle exit is disabled when a screen reader is on. In the swipe mode the buttons faded out are removed from the accessibility tree, so focus cannot land on an invisible control.
- Announcements: entering swipe mode, every base change, comparison start (live, once), every side change, the skipped-layers notice, over-zoom, coverage, offline and error pills (`accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` on Android, `announceForAccessibility` on iOS, once when they appear); the download start, each 25% step and the outcome come from the screen (there is no open sheet to speak).
- Focus order on the map: the centred top control ("Terminé" with the indicator while swiping, or "Quitter la comparaison" in a comparison), the left then right side tag, then the grabber (comparison), capsule (globe, overlay), download button, bubble content when open, locate arrow, legend, then the rest.
- Dynamic Type: bubble and card wrap; on-map pills and tags truncate at the tail. No clipped text at AX3 in the bubble or the card.
- Contrast: bubble and card text on `mapPanel` (verified by the glass tests in both schemes); pills and tags on `mapControl.glass` (`visual-tokens.test.ts`); the hint's white ink on its scrim at 4.5:1 over the lightest basemap (added test); the comparison line, the grabber and the swipe dots over the three reference backdrops at 3:1 non-text (added test); the two dots against the capsule glass at 3:1 (added test); the lightened parcel outline over each old base at 3:1 (added test; tune the 0.6 factor against it); the receded window against the canvas (shadow in light, ring in dark) at 3:1 non-text (added test). Colour is never the only carrier: unavailable layers have a dimmed dot and a sentence; the state of Parcelles has a helper sentence.
- Reduced motion: no wipe animation (instant result), no spring-back, no immersive scale or fades (the final state is shown at once), the line jumps to the centre (and springs back instantly after a drag), tags, pills, hint and chip appear without fade.
