Move the offline-area hook to per-layer packs: the policy decides the layers, the estimator gives the honest weight, the progress is weighted and names the running layer, extra layers fail alone, and the real size is stored.

Commits go to the current branch only (no new branch, no merge, no force push). SQLite schema stays 5. `src/map` keeps 100 % statements and lines; `src/hooks` and `src/storage` keep their floors.
