Purpose: SVA-05 and ROADMAP criterion 2 (pre-filled when online, absent without error offline). D-10, D-11, D-20.
Output: draft patcher conditions actions, `useWeatherPrefill` with its context, the parcel-centre handoff from the selection screen.
</objective>
