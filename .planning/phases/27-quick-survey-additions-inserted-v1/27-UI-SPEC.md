# Phase 27 - UI Design Contract

> Amended on 2026-10-12 by the owner's simulator check (D-32 to D-36, see `27-UI-REWORK-NOTE.md`): the summary rows, the Contexte and Parcelles pages, the note card and the weather card mention below are superseded where they differ.

> Visual and interaction contract for the "Conditions" section (weather and survey note), the per-factor "Douteux" strip with its note, and every place they are displayed (survey summary, score page, community survey, parcel history, own-survey search, PDF). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Sources: 27-CONTEXT.md (D-01..D-18, locked; D-15..D-18 are the UI decisions), ROADMAP Phase 27, CLAUDE.md, charter section 13 and `direction-visuelle-12-2.md`, 25-UI-SPEC.md (structure and tone), the in-repo UI code (`SurveyContextScreen`, `FactorDetailScreen`, `FactorPager`, `SurveyScoreScreen`, `SurveyDetailScreen`, `CommunitySurveyScreen`, `HistoryList`, `ui/*`, `app/survey-pdf/*`).

> No question was put to the owner: D-15..D-18 and the charter answer everything. The four choices that are genuinely open visually are recorded as U-xx decisions at the end and listed under "Open for owner" (each can be vetoed cheaply).

Fixed sizes (all multiples of 4 except hairlines and native control sizes):
- Strip row: `minHeight` 52. The "Ajouter une note" link target: 44 high.
- Weather field: `AppField` default height 48 (`brandComponentTokens.field.minHeight`), radius `brandRadius.field` (18). Weather field `minWidth` 128 and `flexBasis` 128 with `flexGrow` 1, in a wrapping row: two per row at 320 pt wide (2 x 128 + 12 = 268 within 288), one per row once text is large.

- Native `Switch`: platform size (about 51 x 31 on iOS, 52 x 32 on Android); never the touch target (the row is, see 2.2).

Exceptions: 1 pt hairlines; native control size of `Switch`; `AppField` input padding inherited (`horizontalPadding` 16, `verticalPadding` 10). Touch targets never below 44.

## Color

All values come from the theme; light and dark switch with the system only. The PDF is always light.

| Role | Token | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `ScreenFrame` canvas + halo (`colors.canvas`, `visual.backdrop`) | Page background of the context page, the factor pager page, the score page |
| Secondary (30%) | `visual.glass.cardFill` / `cardBorder` (`AppCard variant="glass"`), `componentColors.field.background` / `border` for inputs | Weather card, note card, summary card, read-only note card, inputs |
| Accent (10%) | `visual.accentText` | Reserved list below |
| Warning (doubt, only) | `colors.warningSoft` fill + `componentColors.notice.warningBorder` hairline, `onSurface.warning` text and glyph, `colors.ochre` switch track | The "Douteux" state, nothing else (see list below) |
| Destructive | `onSurface.danger` | Out-of-range message under a weather field (text), and the "Remplacer" button of the refill confirmation (native `Alert` destructive style). Nothing else in this phase is destructive |

Accent reserved for (exhaustive):
1. The note links of the strip: "Ajouter une note", "Modifier la note", "Fermer la note" (with their `create-outline` / `chevron-up-outline` glyph).
2. "Lire la suite" / "Réduire" on the folded note (summary, score rows read-only, community rows).
3. "Réessayer" in the weather unavailable line.
4. "Remplir à nouveau" in the weather edited line.

Not accent (charter 13.4): chips (sky chips use the neutral active chip), chevrons, field borders, the switch (its ON colour is the warning hue because the state it sets is a flag, not a success).

Warning (`Douteux`) is used for exactly these things, in this phase:
1. The ON state of the strip row: soft fill `colors.warningSoft`, hairline `componentColors.notice.warningBorder`, label text `onSurface.warning`.
2. The `Switch` ON track `colors.ochre` (thumb white, the platform default). OFF track `colors.divider`.
3. The `AppChoiceChip variant="status" tone="warning"` badge "Douteux" on score rows and community rows.
4. The PDF badge (palette `warning` / `warningSoft`, see 4.3).

Other colour rules:
- Titles and values `colors.textPrimary`, meta `colors.textSecondary` (verified at 4.5:1 on the glass fill and canvas by the existing tests). Placeholder `colors.textSecondary`.
- Offline and "weather unavailable" lines are calm information: glyph `cloud-offline-outline` in `colors.textSecondary`, text `colors.textSecondary`. No danger glyph, no tinted box, no red (D-11: absent without error).
- The prefilled line glyph is `cloud-done-outline` in `visual.glass.iconTint`; the loading line uses a 16 pt `ActivityIndicator` (small, `colors.textSecondary`).
- Factor tones on score rows (terracotta 0 to 2, ochre 3, high tone 4 to 5) are unchanged and independent from the doubt badge: a doubt never recolours points, ring, bar or band (D-13, D-14). The doubt badge uses the same ochre family as the 3-point tone, so it is always a labelled pill, never a bare ochre dot or bar.
- No `borderWidth` on a gradient view; no `borderCurve`.
- Contrast: `onSurface.warning` on `colors.warningSoft` and on the glass fill are the pairs already measured (about 5:1 and above) for notices and the IBP mid badge. To add to `visual-tokens.test.ts` (both schemes): `colors.ochre` against the glass card fill and against `warningSoft` for the switch track (3:1 non-text). If it fails in one scheme, remove the soft fill in the ON state of that scheme (keep the hairline and the label colour); do not change the track colour and do not rely on colour alone (the label "Douteux" and the thumb position carry the state).

## 1. "Conditions" section (S1)

### 1.1 Placement and entry

`SurveyContextScreen` today: tall map card, parcels list, then the method card (`ScoringContextEditor`). The Conditions section is the LAST block, after the method card, separated by `lg` (24). Reasons: the cas picker is required to submit and must not be pushed down; Conditions is optional; the keyboard fields sit at the bottom of the scroll where they are easiest to bring into view (U-01).

Entry from the summary: the summary `ConditionsCard` (3.1) navigates to `surveyContext` with `section: "conditions"`. The screen then scrolls to the section top (measured with `onLayout`; `scrollTo({ y, animated: !reduceMotion })` once, after the first layout, never again on re-render). Opening the context page from its own row ("Contexte et parcelles") does not scroll.

The section header is `AppSectionHeader` with title `fr.conditions.title` ("Conditions") and subtitle `fr.conditions.subtitle`. Below it two `AppCard variant="glass"` blocks, gap `smd` (12): the weather card, then the note card. Entrance: the section joins the page's existing `EntranceView` stagger (no extra animation).

### 1.2 Weather card (edit mode)

Top to bottom inside one glass card (padding 16, inner gap `smd`):

```
Météo                                                  (13 SemiBold, header role)
[status line: glyph | sentence | optional link]       (13 Regular)
Ciel                                                   (13 SemiBold)
[chip][chip][chip] ... wrapping, 8 gap                 (8 sky chips)
[ Température  [ 14,5   °C ] ] [ Vent  [ 12  km/h ] ]
[ Pluie sur 24 h [ 2   mm ] ] [ Heure  [ 14:30    ] ]
Source : Météo-France                                  (13 Regular, only when a value comes from the provider)
```

Sky chips (`SkyChips`): 8 single-select `AppChoiceChip` (`variant="choice"`, 44 high, wrap, gap 8). Order and labels: Dégagé, Peu nuageux, Nuageux, Couvert, Brouillard, Pluie, Orage, Neige (`fr.conditions.sky`). Tapping the active chip clears the sky (the value is optional). Each press: `feedback.selection()`. The group has `accessibilityLabel` "Ciel du jour"; chips keep role button with `selected` state. The provider's own sky code is mapped by the planner onto these 8 codes; a code the map does not know leaves the sky empty (never a wrong label).

Weather fields (`WeatherFields`): four `AppField` in a wrapping row (`flexDirection: "row"`, `flexWrap: "wrap"`, gap 12, each `flexBasis 128` and `flexGrow 1`). The unit is a trailing suffix inside the field (13 Regular, `colors.textSecondary`, hidden from accessibility); the label is above, 13 SemiBold. Constraints (the same bounds go in `SurveyUpsertDto`):

| Field | Label | Unit | Keyboard (iOS / Android) | Accepted | Placeholder | Range message |
|-------|-------|------|--------------------------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Temperature | Température | °C | `numbers-and-punctuation` / `numeric` | one decimal, comma or point, minus allowed (`parseFiniteNumberInput`) | ex. 14 | Entre -40 et 55 °C |
| Wind | Vent | km/h | `decimal-pad` / `numeric` | integer or one decimal, 0 to 350 | ex. 12 | Entre 0 et 350 km/h |
| Rain over 24 h | Pluie sur 24 h | mm | `decimal-pad` / `numeric` | one decimal, 0 to 1000 | ex. 2 | Entre 0 et 1000 mm |
| Observation time | Heure | none | `numbers-and-punctuation` / `numeric` | "1430", "14:30", "14h30" read as 14:30, normalised to `HH:mm` on blur; 00:00 to 23:59 | ex. 14:30 | Écrivez l'heure ainsi : 14:30 |

- The observation time is the time of day on the survey's date. Only the time is typed; the date part is kept by the draft (the planner stores the full timestamp). It defaults to the time the parcel was chosen (D-10, D-11); a catch-up fill keeps that time, never the reconnection time.
- An out-of-range or unreadable text shows the range message under the field (13 Regular, `onSurface.danger`, only after blur, `AppField error`), is not written to the draft, and never blocks saving, leaving the page or submitting. Leaving the page with an invalid text silently keeps the last valid value.
- iOS number pads have no Return key: the keyboard is dismissed by dragging the page (`keyboardDismissMode="on-drag"`) or tapping outside (`keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`). Android uses `returnKeyType="next"` from field to field and `"done"` on the time.
- A cleared field stores no value (null). Any change by the observer, including clearing, marks that value as edited by hand (see 1.3).

### 1.3 Weather states

One status line sits under the card title, always present, `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` (Android) and announced once through `AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility` on iOS when the text changes while the page is focused. It never contains the word "erreur", never uses a danger colour (D-11).

| State | Condition | Status line (glyph, text) | Fields |
|-------|-----------|---------------------------|--------|
| No parcel | The survey has no parcel yet | `partly-sunny-outline`, "La météo se remplit toute seule une fois la parcelle choisie. Vous pouvez aussi la saisir." | Empty, editable |
| Loading | A parcel exists, online, the request is in flight, no value yet | 16 pt spinner, "Recherche de la météo…" | Empty and editable. A value typed meanwhile is kept and flagged edited (the answer never overwrites it) |
| Prefilled | At least one value came from the provider and none was edited | `cloud-done-outline`, "Préremplie d'après Météo-France pour l'heure indiquée." | Filled, editable. Values appear with a 160 ms fade (no layout animation) |
| Edited by hand | At least one value was edited or cleared by the observer and the provider also supplied values | `cloud-done-outline`, "Préremplie d'après Météo-France, modifiée par vous. Vos valeurs ne seront pas remplacées." + trailing link "Remplir à nouveau" | Editable. Only the values the observer touched are protected; an empty untouched value can still be filled by a late answer (per value, U-02) |
| Manual only | The observer typed values and the provider supplied none | `create-outline`, "Saisie par vous." | Editable |
| Offline, empty | No network, nothing to show | `cloud-offline-outline`, "Pas de connexion. La météo se remplira quand le réseau reviendra, vous pouvez aussi la saisir." | Empty, editable. No error, no button, no toast. When the network returns the section fills by itself for the survey's time (D-11) and moves to Prefilled |
| Unavailable | Online, provider down (clean 503 from the Cortege API) or no data for this point and time | `cloud-offline-outline`, "La météo n'est pas disponible pour le moment. Elle se remplira plus tard, vous pouvez aussi la saisir." + trailing link "Réessayer" (accent, 44 pt target) | Empty, editable. The app retries by itself on network change and on returning to the app; the link retries now. Still no error styling |

- "Remplir à nouveau" (edited state only): opens a native `Alert` titled "Remplacer vos valeurs ?", message "Les valeurs que vous avez modifiées seront remplacées par la météo de Météo-France.", buttons "Annuler" (cancel) and "Remplacer" (destructive style). Confirming clears the edited flags, refetches for the survey's time and shows the Loading state. This is the only way back to the provider's values (U-03).
- Weather never blocks anything: Continue, Terminer le relevé, submit, sync and PDF work with every weather state, including an empty one and an invalid typed text.
- "Source : Météo-France" (13 Regular, `colors.textSecondary`) shows under the fields whenever at least one value is still the provider's, in Prefilled and Edited states. It is the attribution line; the planner confirms the licence wording with the research (D-08).

### 1.4 Survey note card

```
Note du relevé                                         (13 SemiBold)
[ multiline field, min 112, max 240, placeholder ]
Visible par les membres qui peuvent lire ce relevé.    (13 Regular)           123 / 1000   (counter, right)
```

- `NoteField`: `AppField` with `multiline`, `textAlignVertical="top"`, `maxLength={1000}`, `autoCapitalize="sentences"`, `autoCorrect`, `spellCheck`, `accessibilityLabel` "Note du relevé", `accessibilityHint` from the catalogue. Placeholder `fr.conditions.note.placeholder`.
- Counter: `n / 1000` right-aligned on the helper line (13 Regular `textSecondary`). From 900 characters the counter takes `onSurface.warning` and becomes an `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"`; at 1000 it stays warning (the field simply stops accepting). It wraps under the helper at large text sizes.
- The helper line states D-06 once ("Visible par les membres qui peuvent lire ce relevé."), so the observer knows the note is not private.
- Autosave: written to the draft through `useSurveyDraftPatcher` on each change (debounced 400 ms, flushed on blur and on unmount). The existing autosave line of the form is not touched.
- Multiline text: the keyboard Return inserts a new line; the page keyboard rules of 1.6 apply.

### 1.5 Read-only (after submit, and any non-editable survey)

`canEditSurvey` false: all fields, chips, links and counters disappear; the same two cards show plain text (U-04):
- Weather card: `AppGroupedList`-style label / value rows (existing component), one per value that exists: Ciel, Température, Vent, Pluie sur 24 h, Heure. Rows with no value are not drawn. If the weather is entirely empty the card shows one line "Météo non renseignée." (13 Regular, `textSecondary`). Under the rows one source line: "Source : Météo-France." / "Préremplie d'après Météo-France, modifiée par l'observateur." / "Saisie par l'observateur." according to the provenance (no link, no button).
- Note card: the note as plain text, 15 Regular, `selectable` (long press copies), full length, with the title "Note du relevé". Absent note: the card is not drawn.
- Both absent: the whole section collapses to its header plus one line "Aucune condition renseignée." (13 Regular).
- The existing read-only notice of the summary ("Ce relevé est désormais en lecture seule.") covers the lock; no extra lock glyph per field.

### 1.6 Keyboard, focus and scrolling (context page)

- The page scroll view forwards `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`, `keyboardDismissMode="on-drag"`, `automaticallyAdjustKeyboardInsets` (iOS). Android relies on the window resizing for the keyboard; the focused field and, for the note, its counter line must end above the keyboard (verify in the Android pass of Phase 37, charter 13.9).
- On `onFocus` of any Conditions field the page scrolls it into view with its label (measured `y`, `animated` unless Reduce Motion).
- No field takes focus automatically when the page opens, including when arriving through the summary card.
- Focus order for screen readers: header, status line, sky group, the four fields, source line, note label, note field.

## 2. Strip under each factor (S2)

### 2.1 Placement

Inside `FactorDetailScreen`, the page order becomes: input card, score line, **strip**, help link. The strip never moves the input or the score line (they stay first), so the field-entry ergonomics of Phase 12 are untouched.

Interaction budget (phase 12: about 40 interactions per survey): the strip is passive. A user who never uses it performs the same taps and swipes as before: zero extra taps, zero extra screens, no modal, no prompt, no first-use tip, no badge to dismiss, no change to the pager gesture, the A to J letter strip, the progress ring, the "next incomplete factor" shortcut or the finish rules. A doubt costs one tap; a note costs one tap to open, then typing. The strip is not counted in factor completion (a factor with a doubt and no input is still "À remplir").

### 2.2 Anatomy (closed, the default)

One row, `minHeight` 52, no card chrome of its own (flat on the page, so it reads as secondary next to the glass input card and score line), horizontal padding `xs` (4) like the help link:

```
[ Douteux  (switch) ]                        [ create-outline  Ajouter une note ]
```

- Left: the label "Douteux" (15 SemiBold, `colors.textPrimary`) and the native `Switch`. The WHOLE left group is one `AppPressable` (`disableScale`, `disableRipple`, minimum 48 high, `accessibilityRole="switch"`) that toggles; the `Switch` inside is `pointerEvents="none"` and `accessible={false}` (so the target is the row, never the 31 pt control). Toggling calls `feedback.selection()` and writes at once to the draft (no debounce).
- Right: the link "Ajouter une note" (13 SemiBold, `visual.accentText`) with a leading `create-outline` 18 glyph, an `AppPressable` of 44 minimum height, `accessibilityRole="button"`, `accessibilityState={{ expanded }}`.
- When the row does not fit on one line (text above 1.3x, or a narrow phone) the row wraps (`flexWrap: "wrap"`, row gap 4): the switch group on the first line, the link on the second, left aligned. No text is truncated.
- Default values: doubt OFF ("Sûr" is the implied default and is never labelled), no note. Nothing else is drawn.

### 2.3 States

| State | What shows |
|-------|-----------|
| Closed, no doubt, no note | The row above, nothing else |
| Doubt ON | Row gets the warning tint: fill `colors.warningSoft`, 1 pt hairline `componentColors.notice.warningBorder`, radius 22, vertical padding 12 and horizontal padding 16; label in `onSurface.warning`; switch track `colors.ochre`. The row grows by 24 pt (padding), nothing around it moves except what is below. Colour is not alone: the label, the thumb position and the switch state are read aloud |
| Closed, note present | The link reads "Modifier la note" (`create-outline`). Under the row, the note as a preview: 13 Regular, `colors.textSecondary`, 2 lines, tail truncation; the preview is also pressable (opens the note) |
| Open | Link becomes "Fermer la note" (`chevron-up-outline` 18). Below the row a `NoteField`: label "Note du facteur", `multiline`, `maxLength={300}`, min height 96 / max 144, counter `n / 300` right (same 900-style rule at 270 characters), the D-06 visibility helper "Visible par les membres qui peuvent lire le relevé." The field takes focus when opened by the link; opened by the preview it also takes focus |
| Open and then page change | The pager unmounts the page (only the active page is mounted): text is flushed to the draft first, the strip is closed when the page is shown again, with the preview if a note exists |
| Doubt toggled with the note open | The warning tint appears around the switch row only, not around the field |

- Closing: the "Fermer la note" link, or leaving the page. Blur alone does not close the field (so dismissing the keyboard never makes the field vanish). An empty note that is closed leaves no trace.
- Open/close reveal: `FadeIn` 240 ms (`brandMotion.durations.base`) on the field, `LinearTransition` on the strip container, both `.reduceMotion(ReduceMotion.System)`; under Reduce Motion it appears and disappears at once. The warning tint changes without animation.

### 2.4 Keyboard, focus and the pager

- The page scroll view of the pager (each page is its own vertical `ScrollView`) gets `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`, `keyboardDismissMode="on-drag"` and `automaticallyAdjustKeyboardInsets` (iOS). The focused note field and its counter must be fully visible above the keyboard at 320 x 568 (iPhone SE class) and on a 360 x 640 Android window; on Android the field scrolls itself into view on focus.
- While the keyboard is visible the floating bottom bar (A to J strip, round "next" button) and the "Terminer le relevé" pill row are hidden (opacity 0, `pointerEvents="none"`, same frame, no layout change, 160 ms fade, none under Reduce Motion) on both platforms, and the page bottom padding drops to `md` (16). They return when the keyboard goes. This stops the bar riding on top of the keyboard (as Android's search tab already does with `tabBarHideOnKeyboard`) and gives the multiline field the room it needs.
- Any page change (swipe, letter tap, "next") calls `Keyboard.dismiss()` first. The horizontal paging does not start while a text field is being edited by a drag inside it (native behaviour of the inner multiline).
- Focus order for screen readers: factor input card, score line, "Douteux" switch row, note link, note field (when open), help link.

### 4.4 Page budget

| Case | Added height | Effect |
|------|--------------|--------|
| No weather, no note, no doubt | 0 | Identical PDF to today |
| Weather only (common case) | about 73 px (24 + 20 + 10 + one 19 px row) | Under a tenth of a page |
| Maximal survey note (1000 characters) | at most 261 px (weather 2 rows + 13-line note) | The block is never split; it moves to the next page whole if it does not fit |
| Ten maximal factor notes (300 characters each) | at most 10 x 40 = 400 px | At most about one extra page for a survey where every factor carries a maximal note |

The capacity of a page is `(840 - 60 - 40) x 0.92` = 680 px (659 for a draft with its banner). Acceptance (device check, iOS and Android, as the 25.1 constants were measured): a fixture with the maximal note, ten maximal factor notes and ten doubts produces at most one page more than the same survey without them, with no text cut at a footer and no overlap; page count and a page image per platform are recorded next to the other measured constants.

Draft PDFs keep the banner and watermark; the watermark is behind the new text at 0.07 alpha as for the rest. No colour is added to the page chrome.

## Interaction Contract

| Moment | Behaviour |
|--------|-----------|
| Parcel chosen, online | The weather request starts at once for the parcel centre and the current time; no button, no prompt, no toast. The summary card and the context page show the Loading state, then the Prefilled state |
| Parcel chosen, offline | Nothing is requested; Offline state. Back online (network change event or app foreground) the draft fills by itself with the weather of the survey time, per value, without touching what the observer entered |
| Observer edits or clears a weather value or the sky | That value is flagged edited by hand, saved, and never replaced by a provider answer; the status line moves to Edited |
| "Remplir à nouveau" | Native confirmation, then flags cleared and refetch |
| Toggle "Douteux" | `feedback.selection()`, immediate draft write, tint on |

## Accessibility Contract

- Targets: every pressable at least 44 x 44 (strip row 52, switch row 48, links 44, chips 44, summary weather area 52). Hit slop where a glyph is smaller. The native `Switch` is never the sole target.
- Roles and labels: the switch row `accessibilityRole="switch"`, label "Douteux", hint "Signale un doute sur ce facteur. Le score ne change pas.", `accessibilityState={{ checked }}`. Note link `button` with `expanded` state and the factor-named labels of the catalogue; note field labelled "Note du facteur {titre}" / "Note du relevé" with the 300 / 1000 character hint; weather fields labelled with their unit written out (degrés Celsius, kilomètres par heure, millimètres); sky group labelled "Ciel du jour"; decorative glyphs, unit suffixes and the spinner hidden from accessibility.
- Announcements: the weather status line changes are announced once (live region on Android, `announceForAccessibility` on iOS while the page is focused). The note counter is a polite live region only from 90% of the limit (not on every keystroke).
- Focus order: stated per surface (1.6, 2.4). Opening the note field moves focus to it. Closing returns focus to the link.
- Dynamic Type: all text through `AppText`. No fixed heights anywhere (minimum heights only). Strip row wraps instead of truncating from 1.3x; weather fields go one per row; chips wrap; counters wrap under the helper line; the summary weather line takes up to 2 lines (3 at the largest sizes before truncating); factor row notes clamp at 2 or 3 lines with the expand link on read-only rows so a 300-character note never pushes ten rows off the screen. Tested at the largest Accessibility size (about 3x, text capped by `brandFontScaleCaps`): no clipped text, no overlapping row.
- Small screens: layouts verified at 320 pt wide (two weather fields per row at 128 min width), and at 568 pt high for the factor page (input, score line, strip, help link scroll; nothing is fixed except the existing bottom bar, which hides while typing).
- Colour is never the only carrier: Douteux is a word on a chip, a label on a switch and a thumb position; offline and unavailable have a glyph and a sentence; range errors have text.
- Contrast: only theme tokens already measured (`onSurface.warning` on warning and glass surfaces, `accentText` links, `textSecondary` meta) plus the one new pair to add (ochre switch track, see Color). PDF colours come from the light palette and the two new entries are measured by `palette.test.ts` against `paper` and `warningSoft`.
- Reduced motion: no entrance for the new cards beyond the page's own, the reveal of the note field and the fold of the summary note are instant, the weather values appear without fade, the bar hides without fade. All timings carry `ReduceMotion.System`; the only layout transitions use `LinearTransition.reduceMotion(ReduceMotion.System)`.

## Decisions taken in this contract (reasoning)

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|----------|--------|
| U-01 | The Conditions section is the last block of the context page; the summary card is the entry and scrolls the page to it | The cas picker (needed to submit) stays high; Conditions is optional; the card puts the weather in view without a new tab, route or header button, and the fields sit at the bottom where the keyboard handling is simplest |
| U-02 | Overwrite protection is per value, persisted, and a cleared value counts as touched | D-10 ("never overwrites a value edited by hand") and D-11 ("fills in as long as the observer entered nothing") are both met: a late answer fills only untouched values; clearing a wrong prefilled value is respected |
| U-03 | One recovery link, "Remplir à nouveau", in the Edited state only, with a native destructive confirmation | Without it the only way back to the provider values is retyping; it adds no tap for anyone who does not edit. Can be dropped without touching anything else |
| U-04 | After submit the same cards render plain text (label / value rows, selectable note), not disabled inputs | D-03 asks for plain text; disabled inputs look broken and copy poorly. Mirrors the read-only method chips of the same page |
| U-05 | The strip is flat (no glass card) with the switch row and the link on one line; the note field opens in place | Keeps the factor page light for users who never use it (zero extra taps) and avoids a fourth glass card under the input, score line and help link |
| U-06 | The note field stays open until "Fermer la note" or a page change; blur does not close it | Dismissing the keyboard must never make the field disappear while the observer is still writing |
| U-07 | The bottom bar and the finish pill hide while the keyboard is up | The bar is absolutely positioned and would sit on top of the keyboard on Android and eat the room of a multiline field |
| U-08 | Doubt uses the warning family (ochre/soft fill) and a labelled pill, never a bare dot, and never touches points, rings or bands | A flag needs attention, not alarm (no terracotta); ochre is also the 3-point tone, so the word "Douteux" always travels with the colour |
| U-09 | Eight sky values (Dégagé, Peu nuageux, Nuageux, Couvert, Brouillard, Pluie, Orage, Neige) | Enough to describe a field day; fewer than the provider's codes, mapped by the planner; an unknown code leaves the field empty rather than wrong |
| U-10 | The observation time is a typed `HH:mm` text (time of day on the survey's date), not a picker | No date-picker dependency exists and none is worth adding for a rarely edited value; the parser accepts 1430 / 14:30 / 14h30 |
| U-11 | Summary: one `ConditionsCard` (weather line, 2-line folded note, expand in place); empty draft shows an invitation row, empty submitted shows nothing | D-17 asks for one weather line and a folded note; the empty draft row is the discoverable entry when offline |
| U-12 | Score and community rows show a "Douteux" chip and a clamped note; read-only rows expand in place | One shared `FactorsList` serves own, score and community views; notes of 300 characters must not make ten rows unreadable |
| U-13 | Parcel history rows get a one-line note, and the own-survey search says "Trouvé dans la note" | D-06 lists the parcel history; D-07 makes the note searchable, and a result without a visible reason would look like a bug |
| U-14 | PDF conditions block is omitted when empty; badge in the card head, note full width under the body | No empty block on the common PDF; the head badge does not change card height, and a full-width note costs at most 40 px per factor (about one page worst case) |
| U-15 | Typography: three sizes (15, 13, 12), two weights; `AppField` receives the 13 SemiBold label style | Template rule; avoids the legacy ExtraBold field label adding a third weight |
