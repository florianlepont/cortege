# Phase 12.2 Plan 12: Survey detail summary (variant I) Summary

The survey detail summary now carries the second signature surface of the direction: a forest hero score card with the thin gradient numeral, a glow bar that fills once and two glass sub-score tiles that count up, followed by the ten factor bars in a glass card. A successful submit gives a success haptic and a halo pulse on the score card. The OA-46 structure (one bottom button, the three rows, sub-pages, tab bar, `useSurveyDetailData`) is unchanged.

## What was built

- **Score card**: `ScoreCard` takes a new `pulseTrigger` prop. A wrapper `View` keeps `accessible`, `accessibilityRole="summary"` and the label (`a11y.scoreSummary("34 / 50")`, or `Non renseigné` with no score). Inside: `HaloPulse` (radius `forestHero` 28, shadow `visual.forest.shadow`) behind a `ForestCard variant="hero"` with padding 24 holding the caption (label role, `forest.body`), the numeral (`GradientNumeral`, unit `metric.outOfTotal`) in an `Animated.View` that springs from opacity 0 and 8 pt down with `springs.snappy` and `ReduceMotion.System` (final values at once under Reduce Motion), `GlowBar ratio={scoreRatio(total)} animate`, two tiles (`tileFill`, `tileBorder`, radius 16, padding 12) with `AnimatedNumber` values and `scoreCard.outOf({ max })` texts, then the hint. The ten segments and the literal em dash are gone. Text stays left and low, away from the halo at the top right. It is the one animated contour instance of the screen.
- **Catalogue**: `metric.unknown` is "Non renseigné", new `scoreCard.outOf`. No em dash in the file.
- **Styles**: the score card keys were replaced by the ones the new layout reads (`scoreWrap`, `scoreContent`, `scoreCaption`, `scoreNumeral`, `scoreBar`, `scoreTiles`, `scoreTile*`, `scoreHint`); old keys with no reader were deleted. Spacing is on `brandSpacing4`.
- **Factor bars**: `SurveyDetailScreen` memoises `factorPointsFromEntries(data.displayedFactorEntries)` and renders `<AppCard variant="glass"><FactorBarsChart animate /></AppCard>` right under the score card. Rows, `DetailActions`, `FinishBar`, header and refresh are as they were. The file is 268 lines.
- **Submit feedback**: `useSubmitSuccessPulse(status)` keeps the previous status in a ref and a counter in state; when the previous status is draft or error and the new one is submitted or synced it calls `feedback.notify.success()` and increments. Nothing on first render, none for submitted to synced, one for a retry (error to synced) and for a second submit after a return to draft.
- **Map card**: `ParcelMapCard` renders `<ContourLines animated={false} />` as the first child of both wrappers (button and image), before `ParcelMap`; nothing new is above the map. The card clips to radius 22 and has the glass border and fill. Labels and roles are unchanged. `SurveyContextScreen` and `CommunitySurveyScreen` still use it.
- **Photos and header**: `PhotosStrip` is a glass card (fill, border, shadow from `visual.glass`, radius 22, padding 16, gap 12); thumbnails, add button and accessibility are untouched. `SummaryHeader` title uses `brandTypography.screenTitle`, the status line `brandTypeScale.footnote` in `textSecondary` (status word in Jost SemiBold).
- **Tests**: `ScoreCard.test.tsx` (hero variant, numeral, bar ratio 0.68, tiles and texts, tile width, summary label, null score, hint cases, pulse layer props, entrance start values and spring config, Reduce Motion), `useSubmitSuccessPulse.test.ts` (six transitions), `ParcelMapCard.test.tsx` (contours first with `animated={false}` in both wrappers, clip and glass style, roles and labels), `SurveyDetailScreen.test.tsx` (new, the screen had no test: bars card right under the score card, one bottom button, three rows, pulse trigger and haptic on submit and not on mount).

## Decisions and notes

- **Contour placeholder visibility (device only)**: not checked here (no simulator). MapLibre may paint an opaque background as soon as the map mounts, in which case the layer is harmless but invisible; if so it only shows while the map view is not yet laid out. To be looked at in the batch 2 phone check, with the map in airplane mode.
- **Numeral spike**: this is the first screen using `GradientNumeral` in gradient mode. If the SVG text misrenders on the iPhone, set `NUMERAL_RENDER_MODE` to `"solid"` (one constant); the width of the SVG comes from `numeralGeometry`.
- **Tile count-up width**: the tile value is a read-only `TextInput`; I fixed its width from the final digit count (11 pt per digit for Sora-SemiBold 16). If the digit width differs on device, the constant `TILE_DIGIT_WIDTH` in `ScoreCard.tsx` is the one to tune.
- **Pulse timing**: a submit that is queued offline (status stays draft until the sync result) pulses when the status actually leaves draft, which is the observed success, not the tap.
