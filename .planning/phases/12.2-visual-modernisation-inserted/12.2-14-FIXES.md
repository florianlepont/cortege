# 12.2-14 fixes after the owner's batch 2 check

## Mes Relevés and search (D-21, D-22, D-23)

Owner reply, after testing batch 2 on the iPhone. Mes relevés: "vraiment cool", a nice animation on tap, "un peu en mode vague verte", and the page "manque d'un élément accentué, un peu terne et palote". Search: "vraiment cool", but the community tiles are not the same size as the "Mes relevés" tiles and must take their size. Four commits, one per logical change. The halo on every screen (D-19) is a separate later fix: no `ScreenBackdrop` and no header change here.

### 1. Community rows use the Mes Relevés box (d12c150, D-23)

- **Found:** `CommunityRow` had its own card styles (ring directly in the card, no 40 pt ring column, no 4 pt accent bar, a one-line meta instead of the status line that holds a 28 pt chip, title colour `textStrong` instead of `textPrimary`). With the same padding it was about 10 pt shorter than a "Mes relevés" row, and its content sat at different offsets.
- **Changed:** new `SurveyRowFrame` (`survey-list/SurveyRowFrame.tsx`) holds the glass card, the accent bar, the ring column, the title and the status line, from the styles of `row-styles.ts`. `SurveyRow` and `CommunityRow` both render it and only pass their content (own: ring, thumbnail, status chip and date, sync support line; community: ring, author and date). The status line has `minHeight = ROW_STATUS_MIN_HEIGHT` (28 = two 5 pt paddings, the hairlines, and one `meta` line), so a text-only second line cannot make a row shorter than one with a chip. `CommunityRow` keeps its testID, its accessibility label and its open action, and lost its private styles.
- **Tested:** `CommunityRow.test.tsx` renders a `SurveyRow` and a `CommunityRow` and asserts the card, accent bar, ring column, text column, title line, title text and status line resolve to identical styles; it also pins the 44 pt minimum, the 40 pt ring column and the 28 pt status line.
- **Device only:** that the two lists really look the same height next to each other (a community row with a two-line title grows like an own row does), and Dynamic Type at large sizes (the chip and the meta text scale with different caps).

### 2. RipplePressable, the green wave (394ba7d, D-21)

- **Found:** list rows had a plain pressed fill (`surfaceSoft`), no motion.
- **Changed:** new primitive `ui/RipplePressable.tsx`, a `Pressable` that draws a moss circle from the touch point (`nativeEvent.locationX` and `locationY` of `onPressIn`) that grows to the farthest corner in 420 ms with the decelerate bezier of `brandMotion.easings` while it fades out (accelerate bezier, so it stays visible while it spreads and goes at the end), clipped to the surface radius by an absolutely positioned layer (`overflow: hidden`). It is drawn once at the radius of the surface diagonal and only moved with `translateX`, `translateY` and `scale` on the UI thread (no layout animation, no `useNativeDriver: false`, no `LayoutAnimation`). The press is untouched: `onPress` is the caller's function, passed through, and the wave starts in `onPressIn` after the caller's own `onPressIn`. Under Reduce Motion the circle sits at the centre at full size and only its opacity fades in 240 ms (a highlight, not travel; it opts out of `ReduceMotion.System` for that fade only). The layer is hidden from accessibility. Colour: `theme.visual.pressWave` (moss tint `rgba(137, 163, 58, 0.26)` light, `rgba(155, 194, 106, 0.22)` dark, held in `visual-tokens.ts` as `pressWaveFill`). Timings in `brandMotion.pressWave`. `AppPressable` is unchanged.
- **Tested:** `RipplePressable.test.tsx` covers the geometry (`computePressWave`: starts at the touch point, reaches the farthest corner, clamps a touch outside the surface, falls back to the centre without a touch point, Reduce Motion), the animation calls (420 ms, decelerate grow, fade to 0, first frame visible and small, restart from a new touch point), no effect on the press (same `onPress`, fires at once, caller's `onPressIn` called, no wave before the first layout) and the Reduce Motion path (one 240 ms fade, no scale change). `visual-tokens.test.ts` checks primary and secondary row text keep 4.5:1 under the wave at full strength in both schemes. `motion.test.ts` stays green.
- **Device only:** whether the wave reads as a wave and not as a flash (its strength is one token, `pressWaveFill`, and its speed `brandMotion.pressWave`); that `locationX` and `locationY` are relative to the row, not to a child, on iOS and Android (the layer on top is the hit target on purpose, so the rows' children must not be pressable); how it looks while starting a swipe (the wave plays on touch down, the swipe then takes over).

### 3. Rows press with the wave (1d98241, D-21)

- **Changed:** `SurveyRowFrame` renders `RipplePressable` instead of `Pressable`, so Mes Relevés rows and both kinds of search rows (own and community) get the wave. The pressed fill and its `surveyCardPressed` style are removed. The swipe to delete (`Swipeable`), the selection haptic, the accessibility label and actions, the testIDs and the 44 pt minimum height are unchanged. The recent surveys of Accueil (D-20c) are not built yet; they take `RipplePressable` the same way.
- **Tested:** `SurveyRow.test.tsx` and `CommunityRow.test.tsx` check each row carries one wave layer, that a touch down does not open the survey, and that the pressed fill is gone.
- **Device only:** the wave next to the swipe action, and on a selected row (the selected tint is kept).

### 4. Forest summary card on Mes Relevés (05e0b3c, D-22)

- **Found:** the page had a large title, two glass figure tiles and glass rows: everything pale, nothing accented.
- **Changed:** `IntroStats` is replaced by `ListSummaryCard` (`survey-list/ListSummaryCard.tsx`): one `ForestCard` (resume variant, so contour lines drifting only while the screen is focused, one animated instance) with the same two figures (how many surveys, how many to finish) and the same labels, nothing added and no tag pill. Both figures stay on the left of the card on the base forest (the halo core is top right), the total in white and the "à terminer" figure in the light green of the forest card title as the accent, a 1 pt rule between them, 16 pt padding and 16 pt gaps (4 grid). It enters with `EntranceView` (index 0), so it slides in whenever the screen becomes visible like the sections of Accueil. It is the one forest card of the screen. The large title, the section structure and the swipe list are unchanged. The two figures are one spoken sentence for a screen reader, `fr.surveyList.intro.summary` (the unused `IntroStats` styles are deleted).
- **Tested:** `ListSummaryCard.test.tsx` (one forest card of the resume variant with default contours, entrance index 0, the same four texts as before, singular label, spoken sentence, colours from the forest tokens, rule and 4 grid spacing) and a `SurveyListScreen.test.tsx` case (the header carries the card with the real total and to-finish counts, none without surveys).
- **Device only:** whether one compact forest card is enough accent for the page (the halo of D-19 comes with the other fix), how the two figures sit with the large title in light and dark, and the contour drift while the list scrolls.
