# Phase 12.2 Plan 16: Survey wizard and factor inputs (batch 3 part 2) Summary

The wizard sits in the halo frame with glass method cards, score progress segments and a transparent floating bar whose "Continuer" is the glass button. The segmented, slider and counter inputs take the chip and score tokens with every touch size pinned by tests, and the slider fill leaves the moss that failed graphic contrast on light surfaces.

## What was built

### Task 2: segmented, slider and counter inputs (4b895b8)

- Segmented: segments on `visual.chip.fill` with the `visual.chip.border` hairline; the active segment is the inverted neutral pill (`chip.activeBg`, same colour on its border) with `chip.activeText`. `minHeight` comes from `brandInteraction.hitTarget.min` (44) for every segment, replacing the literal.
- Slider: fill `visual.score.high` (`#728A2D` in light, replaces moss `theme.colors.moss`, D-16), track `visual.score.track`. `TRACK_HEIGHT` 8, `THUMB_SIZE` 28, the 44 pt touch row and the thumb are unchanged. The +/- buttons take the chip fill and hairline (see key decisions).
- Counter: buttons on the chip fill with a 1 pt chip border; `BUTTON_SIZE` 56 is kept for width, height and `minHeight`.
- Typography roles of the input chrome are untouched (UI-SPEC exception). Chips and genus list inputs inherit plan 12.2-07 and are not edited.
- Tests added to the three files: token colours, the 44 pt segment minimum (selected or not), the slider's 8 pt track, 28 pt thumb, 44 pt row and buttons, the counter's 56 pt buttons and value area.

### D-19 halo on the wizard (3e53f81)

`SurveyFormRoute` renders the wizard inside `ScreenFrame`. The route table test of the halo frame gains the `surveyForm` row (one frame, one untouchable backdrop, the screen inside it). The wizard and its scroll view draw no canvas of their own any more.

### Task 1: wizard refinement and floating CTA (d4a2136)

- Top bar: back or close and the step label keep their 44 pt; the round button is a glass disc (`glass.cardFill` and `cardBorder`). Progress segments (same 4 pt size) use `score.high` and `score.track`.
- Method cards: glass fill, hairline, soft shadow, radius 22 continuous; the selected card has a 2 pt `accentText` border and 1 pt less padding, so both states have the same size (asserted). The selected radio is the inverted neutral dot (`chip.activeBg`) with its check in `chip.activeText` (was `brandColors.white`).
- No tag pill: "Recommandée" is a plain accent word. D-10: no open OA entry concerns the wizard (checked in `docs/user-tests/owner-acceptance.md`).
- Gaps move to the 4 grid (`brandSpacing4`); the question keeps 30 on 35 with the screen title family.
- Bottom bar: the opaque footer is replaced by an absolute, unfilled bar (`pointerEvents="box-none"`) holding `GlassButton` size lg (native `glassProminent` on iOS 26, forest fallback elsewhere), disabled until the step can continue, testID `wizard-continue` kept. Bottom padding is `finishBarBottomPadding(clearance)`, i.e. above the floating tab bar. The scroll content gets a bottom padding of the measured bar height (estimate before the first layout) plus 16 so the last card and the chips never end behind it.
- `SurveyWizardScreen.tsx` is 279 lines. The unused `cta` and `badge` styles are gone.
- New `SurveyWizardScreen.test.tsx` (15 tests): no canvas, 44 pt buttons, score tokens on the progress, title size, bar geometry and no fill, button props and disabled state, content padding from the measured and the estimated bar height, step flow to the parcel map and back, card tokens, equal card sizes, radio colours, no pill, locked method, v3.0 chips.

## Deviations from Plan

### Owner decisions applied (D-19, D-27c, D-28, given after planning)

1. **[D-19] Halo.** Not in the plan. Route wrapped in `ScreenFrame` (3e53f81). The header stays hidden (see key decisions); the frame inset is 0 there.
2. **[D-27c, D-28] Transparent bar and GlassButton.** The plan did not mention the footer; the opaque footer with `AppButton` became a floating unfilled bar with `GlassButton`. No size changed: the button is still the large 50 pt one, and no control is hidden.
3. **No tag pills.** The "Recommandée" badge was a filled rounded pill; now plain text.

- `choiceCard` border `visual.accentText` for the selected state replaces `ctaPrimary`; `progressDone` is `score.high` as planned.
