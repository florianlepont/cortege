# Phase 12.2 Plan 07: Tab bars, chips, section header and page title Summary

Variant I app chrome shared by every screen batch: both tab bar trees (glass JS bar with a moss active dot and fade, native bar keeping Liquid Glass with token tint and Jost label), glass choice and status chips with an inverted neutral active state and a selection haptic, Sora SemiBold section headers and page titles.

## What was built

- `tab-config.tsx`: `buildJsTabBarStyle` uses `visual.tab.background` and `visual.tab.border`, same height and padding, no `position` key. New `JsTabIcon` (Ionicons glyph plus a 4 pt dot; focused dot is `visual.tab.dot` with `dotShadow`, unfocused is transparent; hidden from accessibility). `jsTabScreenOptions` takes `{ reducedMotion }`, sets `animation` to `"fade"` or `"none"`, token tints and the `brandTypography.meta` label font with no fontWeight.
- `JsRootTabs` reads `useReducedMotion()` (startup snapshot) and passes it; the `shouldHideTabBar` per-screen swap is unchanged. `tab-bar.ts` and its test are untouched.
- `NativeRootTabs` takes `visual.tab.activeTint` and passes `tabLabelStyle={{ fontFamily }}`; no background colour (Liquid Glass preserved).
- `AppChoiceChip`: `visual.chip` fill, border and text; active state is `chip.activeBg` with `chip.activeText`; `feedback.selection()` before `onPress`; `minHeight` still `brandComponentTokens.choiceChip.minHeight` (44). The old interactive shadow was dropped (glass pills).
- `AppStatusChip`: `visual.glass.cardBorder` hairline, neutral fill `visual.chip.fill`, success label `brandColors.forest` in light; every tone label checked at 4.5:1 or more in light and dark.
- `AppSectionHeader` title spreads `brandTypography.sectionHeader` in `colors.textPrimary`, subtitle is `meta` in `textSecondary`. `PageTitle` spreads `screenTitle` and sets `maxFontSizeMultiplier={brandFontScaleCaps.title}`.

## Notes for later plans

- `AppChoiceChip` also renders inside the factor inputs of the survey form (FactorChipsInput, FactorGenusListInput) and in Settings: only colours and the haptic changed there, sizes and behaviour stay (D-05). Their existing tests pass unchanged.
- `AppSectionHeader` and `PageTitle` are used on field screens: typography only, no layout or target change.
- The JS tab icon column is a View wrapping icon and dot; it keeps the bar height fixed because the bar's content height is a constant (56).

- **Files modified:** mobile/src/navigation/tabs.test.tsx
- **Commit:** d0a3f1e
